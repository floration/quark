YOUR OWN FONTS (no programming needed)

Put a .ttf or .otf font file here, then in the game: SETTINGS > FONT > REFRESH, and click it.
It is used for all the game's text at once and remembered (quark.cfg: ui_font).
  - Letters, digits and punctuation (ASCII) are drawn; other characters show as "?".
  - A variable font is drawn at its default weight (often Regular or Light): for bold text use a static Bold file.
  - Every font is sized so its capitals are as tall as the default font's (Rubik Bold).
  - A file that can't be read falls back to Rubik with a notice.
Updates never touch your files here.

The real fonts of some games are used by the game-styled entries in that menu when you have them here
(they are never shipped with quark):
  OVERWATCH       big_noodle_titling.ttf / big_noodle_titling_oblique.ttf (BigNoodleTitling*, BigNoodleToo*)
  APEX LEGENDS    Apex-Bold.ttf (or Apex-SemiBold.ttf, Apex-ExtraBold.ttf, any Apex-*.ttf)
  TF2             tf2build.ttf          (also found in your Steam install of Team Fortress 2)
  TF2 SECONDARY   tf2secondary.ttf      (also found in your Steam install of Team Fortress 2)
  HALF-LIFE 2     verdanab.ttf          (also found in your system's fonts: Verdana Bold)
  QUAKE           conchars.lmp, conchars.png or gfx.wad (also found in your Steam install of Quake)
