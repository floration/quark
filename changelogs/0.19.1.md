# quark 0.19.1

## New 1v1 / tourney ruleset: Vanilla
- Every weapon and all movement abilities, no gimmicks. It's first in the ruleset list, so tourneys can be played straight.

## Maps got a lot less bland
- **Every generated map** gets a detail pass:
  - buttresses along the walls with accent caps
  - base trims and cornices
  - pipes along the long walls
  - overhead beams in tall rooms (they double as grapple anchors)
  - floor strips
  - scattered debris
  
  The pass keeps clear of spawns, objectives and the payload track.
- **Foundry:** an upper ring of catwalks linking the raised corners, wallbang window screens in the middle, and gatehouses on the flanks.
- **Harbor:** a walled warehouse with doorways and a roof deck reached by stairs, plus a catwalk across the containers.
- **Temple:** a bridge between the galleries with a little shrine on top, and arcades across both ends.
- **Colosseum:** an inner ring wall with arches on all four sides and pillars at the corners, which breaks the bowl into lanes.
