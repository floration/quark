# quark 0.14.2

## Weapons
- **Grenade launcher removed.** It's back to 8 weapons.
- **Rifle is semi-auto:** one shot per click. A click up to 0.15 s before the rifle is ready is kept and fires as soon as it can.
- **Sniper hip-fire spread is wider,** and your tracer now shows exactly where the shot went. Spread is worked out the same way on your side and on the server, so what you see is what hit.
- **Crossbow** gets a light zoom on alt-fire (70° field of view). It keeps the normal crosshair and has no scope overlay.

## Server list
- The internet-list status on the Join screen has its own line, so it no longer overlaps the title.
- Host menu: "LIST ON INTERNET" is now **PUBLIC**, and "BOT BALANCE" is now **BALANCE BOTS**, so the labels fit.
- When you host, the screen shows whether your game made it onto the internet list (under the port and UPnP status).

Online play needs everyone on 0.14.2 (protocol 10).
