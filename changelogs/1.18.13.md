# 1.18.13

The menus, settings, in-game pages and map editor have a new look (the owner-approved UI redesign). Every setting is kept.

## Menus
- **New title logo**: QUARK is drawn like the app icon, with the Q split in your team colours. Pick another UI font and the title follows it.
- **Main, join, host and vs bots** pages are tidied: BACK always bottom-left, the main action bottom-right, nothing runs off the screen. The server list has column headers and scrolls with the wheel.
- **HOST > ADVANCED**: the network options (UDP port, auto port forward, server log) moved to their own page. Duel's 1V1 / 2V2 is now a TEAMS cycle.
- **Map, mode, ruleset and ladder pickers** are one card grid with descriptions. Maps not built for the mode get one legend line instead of a warning under every card.
- **Custom mode**: one panel on every tab, a new DASH + GRAPPLE tab, and sliders show the real value in the mode (GRAVITY: 22.0 M/S2) instead of a multiplier.
- **Info** pages are larger text in one column and scroll with the wheel; the **leaderboard** and **demos** fit their panels.

## Settings
- **Six tabs**: PLAYER, GAMEPLAY, CONTROLS, VIDEO + AUDIO, CROSSHAIR, HUD, with hints under each setting.
- **Colour pickers** replace every R / G / B slider trio (right-click: the default). The HUD tab picks its element from a dropdown.
- **Right-click a key slot to unbind it** (or click it and press Del).
- In game, the HUD hides while settings is open.

## Text
- Text fits by itself in every font: a label that is too long is first closed up, then shrunk, and only cut with "..." as a last resort. Fonts that ran outside the line are drawn to fit it.

## In game
- The notices under the score bar are one tidy list (no more lines drawn over each other), the scoreboard starts under them, and TOTAL K / D only shows when someone has one.
- The F3 box no longer runs under the score bar; the cooldown captions are readable.
- Pause, vote, queue and the in-game pickers use the new layout; the hide and seek tiebreak no longer covers the scoreboard.

## Map editor
- The side panel is in sections with larger text; EXIT sits with NEW / SAVE / TEST, BAKE LIGHT beside HOST EDIT SESSION.
- A key bar under the 3D view shows what the map is playable as, its counts and the key help, and fits narrow windows.
- Dialogs are centred in the 3D view. Dragging a resize handle towards the horizon no longer flips the face inwards.
