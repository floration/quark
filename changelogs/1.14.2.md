# 1.14.2

(Includes everything from 1.14.1. No macOS build this release: GitHub's macOS build machines were unavailable.)

- Movement back to the original feel: 8 m/s run, the original acceleration.
- Rockets fly straight again (they no longer carry your momentum); sticky bombs still do.
- Servers that dropped below 1000 Hz now try the next tick rate up every so often, and step back down if it doesn't hold (waiting longer each time).
- Health bars only show when you can see the player's whole body, and are exactly as wide as their hitbox (no spotting players through walls).
- Payload: when the teams swap sides, the cart starts at the new attackers' end of the track.

## Also in 1.14.1
- Run speed back to 8 m/s; explosions 25% louder.
- New sound kit MOUTH replaces WOODEN; make your own kits in `assets/sfx/custom/<kit>/`.
- Orbital: windows into space (a ringed planet, moons, stars).
- Crown letters on the crown model; queue menu in two columns; the launcher always adds desktop and start menu shortcuts.
