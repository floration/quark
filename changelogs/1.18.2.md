# 1.18.2

A small gameplay update: stronger A/D air strafing, a weaker W-only air turn, a quick dash that recharges faster, a super dash that hops higher the further down you look, a lighter sniper body shot, and six fixes. Network protocol unchanged (42), but the movement changed: play 1.18.2 against 1.18.2 servers, or your own movement will be corrected by the server.

## Movement
- **A/D air strafing is stronger**: with a strafe key held, the air strafe cap is 10% higher (2.3 to 2.53) and air acceleration is 15% higher. Strafe-jumping builds speed faster: about 20.7 m/s after one second from a run instead of 18.2.
- **W-only air turning is 10% slower** at every speed.
- **Quick dash** (duel's QUICK DASH and DASHING BLADES, the BLADES arena) now cuts the dash cooldown to 0.4 s instead of giving a third charge. You have the usual 2 charges.
- **Super dash**: the further down you look, the higher you hop, from half a jump (0.31 m) just past 14.5° down to a full jump (1.22 m) straight down.

## Weapons
- **Sniper**: a body shot does 40 (was 50). A headshot still does 50, so it takes 2 headshots or 3 body shots to kill.

## Fixes
- Tag: players knocked out in one match no longer start the next match already out.
- Payload (Railyard): in team B's attacking rounds, the cart's collision is where the cart is drawn, instead of at the other end of the track.
- The payload cart's shadow no longer gets baked into the cached map lighting (maps re-bake their lighting once).
- See-through parts of custom weapon models now show in first person.
- A fresh announcer line is no longer cut off when 64 sounds are playing.
- The dash bar on the HUD fills over quick dash's 0.4 s cooldown.
