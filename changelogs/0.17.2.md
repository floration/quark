# quark 0.17.2

## For developers: Tracy profiling
- Build with `-DQUARK_TRACY=ON` (optionally `-DQUARK_TRACY_DIR=<tracy checkout>`) and connect the Tracy profiler to see named CPU zones for every part of a frame:
  - event polling, net, input and prediction, world building
  - the shadow, world and dynamic passes, the HUD, 2D flushing, swap and the low-latency wait
  - the hosted server's simulate and snapshot steps, and the audio mixer
- Plots show vertices and bytes uploaded per frame, and profiling builds also print those numbers every 5 seconds.
- Normal builds are unchanged: the zones compile to nothing.
