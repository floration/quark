# 1.18.14

## Settings
- **DEVICES** (VIDEO + AUDIO, bottom right): pick the **sound output** (system default or any device by name; the sound moves there at once), the **microphone** (saved for voice chat later: nothing records yet), the **monitor** fullscreen and borderless open on, and the **refresh rate** for exclusive fullscreen. REFRESH lists them again. A saved device that's unplugged falls back to the default with a notice.
- **EFFECTS VOLUME** and **ANNOUNCER VOLUME** (VIDEO + AUDIO → SOUND, under VOLUME): turn the sound effects (the hitsound too) and the announcer up or down on their own. Right-click a slider: back to 1.00.
