# 1.18.32

A fix for the grapple sound. No protocol change: 1.18.32 plays with 1.18.22 and newer games and servers (protocol 44).

- **Grappling a wall you're standing against no longer spams the sound.** Holding grapple with your face to a wall used to replay the grapple sound every frame, very loudly. Now it grabs once, and at most once a second while you hold the button, like any other grab and release.
