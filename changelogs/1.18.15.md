# 1.18.15

## Servers
- **Dedicated servers with nobody on pause.** With only bots (or no one) on, a dedicated server stops the game: no ticks, no snapshots, the clock stands still, and it uses next to no CPU. The bots' match is dropped, so the first player to join starts at warmup as on a fresh server. Hosting from the game and vs bots are unchanged.

## Hide and seek
- The seeker's game is now sent hiders within **24 m** without a line-of-sight check (was 20 m), so hiders close by never pop in late. Other modes stay at 20 m.
