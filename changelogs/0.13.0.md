# quark 0.13.0

## New mode: DUEL (1v1 or 2v2)
- 1v1 or 2v2, chosen when hosting (Host menu → TEAMS, or `--teamsize 2` on a dedicated server). First to 5 rounds. Anyone beyond the team slots spectates.
- Each life (round) is played under a random modifier for everyone, never the same one twice in a row:
  - **Vampire laser**: lasers only, and damage you deal heals you.
  - **Rockets only**
  - **Knives only**
  - **1 HP**: everyone has 1 health.
  - **Double size**: both players are twice as big, hitboxes included.
  - **Grapple movement only**: no walking, jumping or dashing. The grapple is the only way to move.
- The current modifier is shown under the scoreboard. A stalemate drains health, like VS.
- New map **Crucible**, built for duels: high open ceiling (double-size players fit everywhere), tall pillars to grapple from, short sightlines. Picked by default for DUEL. Use `--mode duel` on a dedicated server (`1v1` also works).

## Time to kill: 2 seconds for every weapon
With perfect aim, every weapon now kills a full-health player in exactly 2.0 seconds:

| weapon | damage | shots to kill | time between shots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rifle | 10 | 10 | 0.222 s |
| Rocket | 35 | 3 | 1.0 s |
| Knife | 40 | 3 | 1.0 s |
| Sniper | 50 | 2 | 2.0 s |
| Laser (beam) | 4 | 25 | 0.083 s |
| Shotgun | 5 × 10 pellets (50 full blast) | 2 | 2.0 s |
| Sticky | 35 | 3 | 1.0 s |
| Crossbow | 50 | 2 | 2.0 s |

## Controls
- Scroll wheel binds fixed: one action per wheel notch (smooth-scrolling trackpads and high-res wheels no longer skip several weapons), and the wheel now works for hold actions like jump or fire.
- Binding an input to an action removes it from any other action, so it no longer triggers two things at once.

## Weapons
- You respawn with the weapon you had selected last (including a weapon picked while dead), instead of the rifle.

- Rockets fly faster (48 m/s, was 32) and now drop slightly (gravity 5 m/s², about a third of the sticky's).

## Internet server list
- Hosted games and dedicated servers can now show up for everyone on the **Join** screen, not just on your LAN. Host menu → **LIST ON INTERNET** (on by default); dedicated servers use `--public`.
- The Join screen lists LAN and internet games together and asks every server directly, so it shows only games you can actually reach, with real ping and player counts.
- Runs on a free Cloudflare Worker, so no VPS is needed. It goes live as soon as the list's address is added to the website.

## Server logging
- Hosts and dedicated servers can write a game log to `logs/`:
  - **Simple**: deaths, map changes, chat, round and match wins.
  - **Verbose**: simple, plus hits and every player's position, velocity, aim, health and weapon at 125 Hz.
  - **Extreme**: everything at the full tick rate (up to 1000 Hz), including inputs, movement timers and projectiles.
- In game: Host menu → **SERVER LOG**. Dedicated server: `--log simple|verbose|extreme` and optional `--logfile PATH`.
- The log is buffered, so even extreme logging stays off the tick's critical path.

Online play needs everyone on 0.13.0 (protocol 8).
