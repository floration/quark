# quark 0.12.1

## Sound
- All sounds rebuilt to be more satisfying and less robotic: weapons have a weighty low "body" plus a filtered crack instead of raw noise, a short room reverb tail, and smooth fades (no clicks).
- Hitsound is now a soft, rounded tick; kills are a two-note chime.
- Footsteps and movement are softer and more muffled.
- Everything is quieter: roughly half the previous loudness, and the default volume is lower (60%).

## Text input
- Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) pastes into any text box: server address, name, port, and chat. Ctrl+C copies the text box's contents.
- Spaces and line breaks around a pasted server address are ignored.
