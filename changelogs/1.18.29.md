# 1.18.29

Settings > HUD now shows your HUD live while you edit it. No protocol change: 1.18.29 plays with 1.18.22 and newer (protocol 44).

- **Live HUD preview.** On the HUD tab the real HUD stays on screen behind a see-through settings panel (in a match with your live values, from the main menu with sample ones). The element you picked is outlined, and while you drag its sliders or change it, it's drawn over the panel so you see it move, resize and recolour as you go. A hidden element shows a dashed outline where it would be.
