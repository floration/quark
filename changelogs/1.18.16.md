# 1.18.16

Two sound changes.

## Sound
- **Hide and seek and tag: everyone hears the locator.** When the seeker (or the tagger) fires the locator, every other player hears its ping from where the seeker stands, in the seeker's sound pack. Before, only the seeker heard it.
- **Three unused announcer lines are gone.** AIRBORNE, TEN SECONDS and FIVE SECONDS were never played and had no recording in any voice; every voice now has 27 lines.
