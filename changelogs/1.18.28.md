# 1.18.28

The hyper (super dash) now launches you 3x higher, to make up for its lower speed since 1.18.7. No protocol change: 1.18.28 plays with 1.18.22 and newer games and servers (protocol 44), but update both: on a mixed game and server a hyper jolts once as it lands where the server says.

- **Hyper hop 3x higher.** Aimed just past the hyper angle you now rise about 0.9 m (was 0.3 m), at 45 degrees 2.4 m (was 0.8 m), and straight down 3.65 m (was 1.22 m, a standing jump). You stay in the air about 1.7x as long, so a hyper carries you about 1.7x as far.
