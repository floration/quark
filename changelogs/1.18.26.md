# 1.18.26

A small fix to weapon switching, plus faster tests on Windows. No protocol change: 1.18.26 plays with 1.18.22 and newer games and servers (protocol 44).

- After you throw your knife (or syringe), your view model and HUD go straight back to the weapon you held before it. For one ping they used to show the rocket launcher first.
- The developers' full test run on Windows takes about 3 minutes 20 seconds instead of 14, so fixes get checked and shipped sooner.
