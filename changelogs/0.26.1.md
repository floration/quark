# 0.26.1

- **Explosions set off nearby stickies**, yours and the enemy's: rockets, stickies, anything with splash. Chain reactions work.
- **Knife:** reach is 2.8 m (was 2.2 m), and its beam now runs all the way from you to the tip.
- **New weapon models.** Each weapon has its own shape:
  - **Rifle:** magazine, barrel and front sight.
  - **Rocket launcher:** flared tube with exhaust rings.
  - **Knife:** guard and long blade.
  - **Sniper:** scope on rings, bolt and muzzle brake.
  - **Laser:** glowing coils and emitter.
  - **Shotgun:** twin barrels with a pump.
  - **Sticky launcher:** drum magazine.
  - **Crossbow:** limbs, string and a bolt.
- **Servers:** the auto-updater puts the shipped maps back before updating, so a stray local change can't block updates.
