# 1.18.31

The hyper (super dash) now sends its speed up off the floor, and dashes aim less steeply up and more steeply down. No protocol change: 1.18.31 plays with 1.18.22 and newer games and servers (protocol 44), but update both: on a mixed game and server, dashes aimed up or down and every hyper jolt once to where the server puts them.

- **Hyper: up to 90% of its speed goes up.** It leaves the floor as if your dash bounced off it: the further down you look, the more of its speed points up, at most 90% looking straight down. Its speed is the same as before (what you carried in + 5), but the part that goes up no longer moves you forward. From a run: about 0.45 m high at 20 degrees down, 1.9 m at 45 degrees, 3.9 m straight down. This replaces the 3x hop from 1.18.28.
- **Dashes aim up at most 40% and down at most 90%.** Looking straight up, 40% of a dash's speed goes up (it was 64%); looking straight down, 90% goes down (was 64%). A standing jump with a dash straight up at its top now peaks about 2.8 m (was 4.15 m).
