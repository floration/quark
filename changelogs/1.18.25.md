# 1.18.25

A behind-the-scenes update to the developers' tests. Nothing changes in the game, and it plays with 1.18.22 to 1.18.24 (protocol 44).

- The automatic map grading is gone: the maps will be designed by hand. Every check that catches a broken map (spawns in walls, unreachable objectives, ladders, stairs, places you can reach out of bounds) stays.
- The full local test run takes about 1 minute 50 seconds instead of about 5, so fixes get checked and shipped sooner.
