# 0.38.0

- **Fixed: self-hosted servers dropping to 250-500 Hz.** The client counted the snapshot gap in ticks, but since 0.36 snapshots go out every other tick. It reported exactly 50% "loss", and the server lowered its tick rate in response. Loss is now counted in snapshots. A new test runs a loopback match with the rate check on and requires 0% loss at 1000 Hz.
- **Your sound pack is heard by everyone**, like your model. Other players hear your guns, footsteps, grapple, hurt sounds and explosions in the pack you picked, and you hear theirs in theirs.
  - All packs are built at startup, so switching costs nothing.
  - Every pack is normalised to the same loudness as STANDARD (measured: within 0.01 dB on every sound), so no pack is louder or quieter.
- **Other players' hurt sounds** are now played (positioned at them, throttled to at most 5 per second).
- **Ranked uses the default player model** for everyone. Model changes are refused, bots spawn standard, and every client draws standard models. Sound packs stay allowed. The ranked screens now mention the default model too.
- Protocol 20: player info carries model and sound pack.
- **Viaduct is now two full loops**, each leg higher than the last:
  - leg 1 is the old course;
  - leg 2 climbs back south along the east edge;
  - leg 3 runs north along the west edge;
  - leg 4 is a high skyway down the middle to a finish above the start.
  
  It has five more checkpoints, beams, hurdles and slaloms. A run now takes about four times as long. Bots can still reach every checkpoint (checked by the tests).
- **Interpolation setting** (Settings → INTERP): adaptive (the default), or a fixed 5-100 ms delay for other players. Ranked always uses a fixed 10 ms.
- **UI text always fits**: any label too wide for its button, toggle, cycle or slider shrinks just enough to fit. This fixes the overlapping sound-pack, HUD toggle, widget editor and opposite-keys labels.
- The ranked fairness notice no longer overlaps the ruleset line.
- **Review fixes**:
  - big servers no longer send snapshots faster than their cap;
  - the delta-base ring survives tick-rate changes;
  - one large reliable event no longer blocks smaller ones;
  - keys held through a menu are no longer fresh presses afterwards;
  - widget labels can't break the file format;
  - `hud.cfg` hot-reload also notices quick edits;
  - control profiles store binds by action name, so they survive future updates;
  - sticky-bomb sounds use their owner's sound pack.
- The website has a 1.0 refresh: stats, a "Make it yours" section, the twelve modes and the ranked fairness rules.
