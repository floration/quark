# 1.18.19

A gameplay feel update: you hear enemies move, taps are never lost, the death screen tells you who killed you, a fallen hide and seek seeker comes back, and empty servers stop playing by themselves. **No protocol change: 1.18.19 plays with 1.18.16 games and servers**, so you can update either one first.

## Movement
- **Short taps always count.** A jump, dash or grapple press shorter than a frame is no longer lost.
- **Tapped jumps buffer like held ones.** A jump tapped up to 0.1 s before you land jumps the moment you land, the same as holding jump. Before, a tap 30 ms early did nothing.
- **Holding dash dashes as soon as it can.** You no longer need a fresh press, and a press up to 0.1 s early is kept until the dash is allowed.
- **Strafe jumping is the same at any frame rate.** At 60 fps you used to lose about 5% speed (11% at 30 fps) against a fast machine; now it's under 0.1%.
- **Fewer corrections online**: a syringe refill and grappling an enemy are now predicted, so your dashes and hook don't jump back a round trip later.

## Combat
- **Rifle body 4.95 -> 5.0** and **shotgun head pellet 2.55 -> 2.6**, so 20 rifle body shots and two point-blank head blasts now kill (they left 1 HP).
- **Crossbow body 50 -> 49** (head still 75): a headshot now saves a bolt. Two body bolts no longer kill; it takes three.
- **Your own rocket splash ramps up smoothly** with height: a rocket at your knees costs about 10 HP instead of 34. Rocket jumps are unchanged.
- **Sniper, crossbow and rocket keep a click up to 0.1 s early** and fire the moment they're ready.
- **After a knife or syringe throw you go back to the weapon you held**, not the rocket.
- **Shots line up better with what you saw**: lag compensation is exact between ticks, and refire is timed by your own commands, so taps at the end of a cooldown fire at once with your aim.
- **Rocket splash hits a player peeking over cover.**
- **Shotgun HEADSHOT** only when at least half the blast hit the head.
- **Knocking someone off the map within 3 s is your kill** (rocket, crossbow knock, grapple pull). Before, nobody got the point.
- **A click right after you respawn no longer plays a shot the server never fires.**
- The bubble stream no longer gives away enemies behind walls.

## Sound and HUD
- **You hear enemies move**: their jumps, landings (louder when hard), dashes, supers and hypers, in 3D. Your own jump, landing and dash sounds no longer drop out when you bunny hop or press jump and dash together.
- **Your hurt sound is calmer**: at most once every 0.15 s and louder for bigger hits. It used to play about 40 times a second under the laser and drown out footsteps.
- **Distant gunfire**: a hidden shooter within 60 m is now heard as a muffled shot from roughly where they are.
- **A red arc round the crosshair** points to whoever hit you, and **enemy rifle shots draw tracers**.
- **Kills**: a red X kill marker, and a killed player you can see drops with a death sound and a red burst. The last hit's sound and number now come before the kill sound.
- **The death screen** shows who killed you, with what, their HP (not in ranked) and RESPAWN IN 1.4.
- The weapon bar now fills over alt-fire refire and the spawn lock. Enemy footsteps are heard on stairs.

## Modes
- **Spectating in team modes**: after you die you watch your killer for 1.5 s, then only teammates.
- **Hide and seek: a seeker who falls or dies is back after 2 s.** Before, the round idled out and every hider scored.
- **Hide and seek ties** go to whoever stayed hidden longest; rock paper scissors only if that's level too.
- **Payload and multi point**: round 1 ends with "TEAM A pushed 57 m - TEAM B must beat it" instead of a ROUND WON that turned into YOU LOSE. Round 2 ends as soon as the result is settled.
- **1v1, duel and tourney**: the break between rounds is 2.5 s (was 4 s), and the calls use player names instead of TEAM A.
- **Team spawns avoid enemies' sight**, not teammates (Summit KOTH: 57% of respawns in sight before, 22% now).
- Killstreaks no longer carry over from warmup or the last match. Multi point can't start on a map without points.

## Bots
- **Play vs bots in 1V1 is one bot**, and DUEL uses the duel team size (both were 2v3).
- **Bomb vs bots on Depot and Lodestone works**: defenders split across the sites, and bots no longer stick on walls there.
- **Bots turn toward whoever hurt them**, even a sniper they couldn't hear, after their reaction time.

## Servers
- **An emptied server stops playing** (not ranked): when a side or everyone leaves, the match waits, and after 10 s gives the side still there a forfeit win or goes back to warmup. VS used to loop "round draw" every 7 s, and a 1v1 leaver handed out rounds.
- Older 1.18.16 clients joining a 1.18.19 server play normally; they just miss the new sounds and markers.
