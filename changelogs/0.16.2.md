# quark 0.16.2

## Main menu
- **The menu background is back.** The menu now has its own backdrop, a slow camera orbit around Crucible. Before, the menu was plain black until you had played a map. Its lighting is baked once on the first start, then cached.

## Website
- **Redesigned** floration.github.io/quark:
  - A download button for your platform, plus every other platform and the full game builds.
  - What the engine does, with tick rate, movement steps and input latency.
  - Every mode with a picture of its map.
  - A live weapon table, generated from the game's source on every release, so the numbers never go stale.
  - A hosting guide, and a collapsible changelog that shows tables and formatting.
  - Works on phones.
