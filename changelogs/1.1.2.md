# 1.1.2

Republishes 1.1.1, whose release build stopped on a flaky bot-aim test. It includes everything from 1.1.1: dash and grapple in hide and seek and tag, and no knife throws in those modes.

- **Bot skill levels are further apart.** Beginner and intermediate aimed almost the same. Against a strafing player with a rifle, the levels now land roughly 3 / 5 / 8 / 14 damage per second (beginner / intermediate / advanced / expert).
