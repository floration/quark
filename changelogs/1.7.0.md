# Quark 1.7.0

## Custom game modes
- Build your own modes in game: **Host > Custom mode > NEW / EDIT**.
  - **General:** the mode it plays as, health, gravity, move speed, jump height, knockback, and dash / grapple / self damage / instagib / vampire / regen toggles. Time, score and round limits are also set here.
  - **Weapons:** turn each weapon on or off and set its fire rate, damage and projectile speed.
  - **Maps:** a map pool that rotates to the next map after every match.
- Presets are saved to `custom_modes/<name>.qmode`. Pick one in the Host menu and host as usual.
- Everyone who joins gets the server's rules automatically. Movement and weapon timing predict exactly, and the server list shows the mode as `[name] server`.
- Map votes on a custom server stay inside its pool and its mode. Ranked always uses the standard rules.

## Fixes
- The laser beam now comes out of the laser model's emitter and follows the HUD viewmodel position, depth and scale.
- Jumping, falling, grappling or dashing into stairs carries you up them like walking does. You no longer bonk off the risers and lose your speed.
- Switching weapons with fire or alt-fire held (e.g. scoped sniper) no longer fires the new weapon. Let go and press again.

## Balance
- Sniper: no spread when unscoped. Hip shots go exactly where the crosshair is.
- Bubble gun: bubbles travel 20% faster (primary 15.6 m/s; the alt-fire stream 8.4 to 24 m/s).
