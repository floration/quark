# 1.15.1

(No macOS build this release.)

- **Spectating** is obvious now: a big SPECTATOR banner under the score box.
- **Custom modes**: every setting shows the standard value and what your mode changes it to (e.g. AIR ACCELERATION 2.20 -> 4.40). Long lists (maps, movement) add columns instead of running off the screen.
- **Discord**: update announcements ping @Update Pings instead of everyone. A #get-pings channel greets new members and lets you pick Update, Game and Other pings.
