# 0.36.0

## Fixes
- **Knife throw:** right-clicking to throw the knife no longer fires the weapon you switch to (the rocket lob) straight away. Let go of the button, then click again.
- **Rocket alt-fire:** the lob now flies 1.4x as fast (70% of rocket speed), with the same 3x gravity.

## Server: less CPU, less bandwidth
- **Snapshot rate:** snapshots are sent at their own rate, separate from the 1000 Hz simulation. The default is 500 per second; `--snaprate N` changes it (1000 = lowest latency, 250 or less = less CPU and upload).
  - Your input is still simulated every millisecond.
  - Interpolation of other players grows by about 2 ms (7.1 → 9.2 ms on the localhost test).
- **Big servers** keep the full 1000 Hz tick and send fewer snapshots instead. Before, the whole server dropped to 250 or 500 Hz above 16 players.
- **Leaner snapshots:**
  - Your own grapple and dash state is only sent while active.
  - Projectile velocity is only sent when it changes.
  - Reliable messages are re-sent once per round trip instead of in every snapshot.
  - Result: 172 → 129 bytes per snapshot. With half as many snapshots, upload per player is about 62% lower (≈1.4 → 0.5 Mbit/s).
- **Snapshot buffers** are reused instead of being reallocated every send.
- **An empty server** sleeps between ticks instead of busy-waiting.
- Benchmark (`quark_tests serverbench`): Harbor, 12 bots + 4 players, 102 → 69 µs per tick at 1000 Hz.

## Protocol
- Network protocol version 19: everyone needs 0.36.0.
