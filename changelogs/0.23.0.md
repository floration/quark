# 0.23.0

- **Fixed: thin gaps along the edges of accent and wallbang blocks.**
  - Last update raised every accent and wallbang face slightly off its block to stop flicker where it meets another material, which opened gaps at the edges.
  - Now only faces that actually overlap a different-material face in the same plane get raised. Everything else sits flush.
- **Drag handles in the map editor.**
  - Selected boxes and zones get a handle on every face; drag one to move that side.
  - Hold Shift to drag the whole object along that axis instead.
  - Spawns, lights and path points get arrows to drag them around.
  - Handles are colored by axis and snap to the grid. They also work when editing together.
- **The editor's 3D view stops at the side panel.** The crosshair, where new things are placed, is now in the middle of what you can see.
- **Choose which modes a map allows.** In the editor, click the mode buttons to allow or block each mode.
  - Dim buttons mean the map is missing something that mode needs, like team spawns.
  - Map menus and votes only offer the allowed modes.
  - Saved as a `modes ffa duel ...` line in the map file.
