# quark 0.7.0

## New weapons
- Shotgun (6): 10 pellets x 9 damage (up to 90), wide spread, 0.85 s between shots. Close range. All pellets that hit the same player count as one hit.
- Sticky launcher (7): lobs sticky bombs that stick to walls and floors (up to 6 out; a 7th removes your oldest). Hold right mouse to detonate them (armed after 0.35 s). 85 damage, 3.4 m blast, same self-damage rules as rockets (10% from below), so sticky jumps work.
- Crossbow (8): fast bolt (90 m/s) with slight drop, 85 damage on a direct hit, no splash.
- Keys 1-8 and the scroll wheel cycle all 8 weapons.

## Other
- Weapon switching is sent as a weapon number instead of button bits (room for more weapons). Protocol version 5.
