# 1.1.1

- **Hide and seek and tag:** dash and grapple now work for everyone, seekers and hiders alike.
- **Fixed: endless knife throws.** Holding alt-fire while clicking fire threw the knife past every check. In hide and seek / tag that meant unlimited throws; in other modes, a second throw while the knife was still away. Throws now follow one rule everywhere: knife in hand, and never in hide and seek or tag. A test holds both buttons and checks that nothing is thrown.
