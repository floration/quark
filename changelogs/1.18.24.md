# 1.18.24

Clearer calls in bomb, payload and FFA, the last item from the gameplay feel review. **No protocol change: 1.18.24 plays with 1.18.16 to 1.18.23 games and servers.**

## Calls
- **Bomb tells you your job at every round start**: "ATTACK: CARRY THE BOMB TO A SITE AND PLANT IT" or "DEFEND: KEEP THEM OFF THE SITES, DEFUSE A PLANTED BOMB" during the freeze.
- **Bomb halftime is announced**: "HALFTIME - SIDES SWITCHED: YOU ATTACK NOW" (or DEFEND).
- **A dropped or picked-up bomb is called** for both sides ("BOMB DROPPED - PICK IT UP" / "THEY DROPPED THE BOMB").
- **Payload checkpoints** play the POINT TAKEN or POINT LOST voice for your side.
- **FFA only calls your own lead changes** ("YOU TOOK THE LEAD" / "YOU LOST THE LEAD"), and the line under the mode title shows your place, like "2ND OF 8".
