# 1.18.24

Bots stop walking into walls and crates, and you can pick the brain your hosted game's bots play.

## Bots
- **No more walking into walls**: bots used to plan straight across a floor through whatever stood on it. Fixed cases include the Depot bomb carrier pushing into the gatehouse wall, Depot yard runs stopping at the perch, and Summit's west spawn walking into the ledge ramp for 4-20 s. Bots now path round walls, crates and posts.
- Bomb carriers and defusers chasing someone no longer think twice as hard every tick.
- Bots spread their looking across server ticks instead of all looking on the same tick after a round restart, so busy servers have smaller tick spikes (Hamlet with 24 bots: worst bot think time per tick down about 25%).
- Summit KOTH bots now get round the hill properly, so they hold it about 30% less than before (they used to stand stuck on it).

## Hosting
- **HOST GAME > ADVANCED > BOT BRAIN**: pick BUILT-IN or any brain file in your `bots/` folder (made with the bot editor). Bots added from the console play it too. A brain with an error says which line and the bots play the built-in brain. Ranked games always use the built-in brain; VS BOTS keeps the built-in brain.
