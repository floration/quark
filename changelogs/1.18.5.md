# 1.18.5

A client performance and polish update: smoother frames, fewer stutters, faster map loads, and a batch of fixes. Network protocol unchanged (42): it plays with 1.18.x games and servers.

## Loading
- **The official maps ship with their lighting already baked.** No more bake on the first join after an update: Midtown 5.3 s → 0.05 s, Lodestone 2.8 s → 0.04 s. The download grows by about 12 MB.
- **Cached map loads are about 2.5x faster** (the light bake's preparation runs on every core).

## Performance
- **Text and HUD drawing is about 7x cheaper per letter** (about 160 µs a frame with F3 open, 40-75 µs for a normal HUD), with exactly the same picture.
- **No more once-a-second stutters**: the HUD's hud.cfg check, the settings page, Latent Sync and F3 no longer do slow system calls during the frame.
- **Sounds never make the game wait** for the audio mixer.
- **Less work between your mouse and the screen**: most of the remaining GPU setup now happens before the late mouse read.
- Health bars, the editor, the netcode and the menus do far less per frame (details in the full notes).

## Audio
- **3D sounds follow you while they play**: an explosion behind you moves to the other ear when you turn, instead of staying where it started.
- **Lower-latency normal audio on Windows 10+** where the sound driver allows it (about 3 ms instead of 10, without exclusive mode).
- **EXCLUSIVE AUDIO works on more devices** (24-bit and float-only devices), and says N/A when the device takes none of its formats instead of BUSY.

## Fixes
- **Kill feed, chat and name events could be lost** when packets arrived out of order. They're now always delivered, in order.
- **The click that brings the game back after alt-tab no longer fires your weapon.**
- **ALT TOGGLE scope**: the view now zooms with the toggle (it only zoomed while the key was held).
- **ULTRA low latency kept its pacing** when the GPU is the bottleneck (it slowly switched itself off).
- **Enemy footsteps no longer play in mid-air** at high frame rates.
- **Killstreak callouts start fresh each match** (a spree carried over to the next match).
- A click just before Esc, chat or a map change no longer fires a stray shot.
- Weapon kick recovers while scoped or with the weapon model hidden (unscoping replayed it).
- Giant / double-size enemies' shots start at their eyes; a spectator sees the watched player's laser and sniper shots.
- F3's input-to-frame number was one frame too high.
- The custom mode editor's PLAYS AS reaches every mode; the console says "unknown setting" for a typo with a value; a possible crash in the map picker after deleting maps.
- Editor: big maps stay smooth while editing (no 30 MB rebuild per keystroke or slider move), and an online edit can no longer change the wrong object when someone else adds or deletes one.
