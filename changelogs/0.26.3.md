# 0.26.3

- **Fixed: whole surfaces (like the floor) sometimes disappeared.**
  - The renderer skips faces that are completely buried inside other blocks. It decided that from only 5 sample points, and corner columns plus something in the middle of the map could cover all 5. The entire floor was then treated as buried and not drawn.
  - It now checks a 9×9 grid, so only faces that really are buried get skipped.
  - Maps re-bake their lighting once.
