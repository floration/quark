# 1.18.20

Quips get their own setting.

## Sound
- **QUIPS on/off and QUIPS VOLUME** (VIDEO + AUDIO > SOUND). Quips are the announcer's hype lines played after a frag. They no longer follow the ANNOUNCER setting or its volume: you can mute the call-outs and keep the quips, or the other way round.
- **Quips are only hype lines.** By default each voice's quips are its HEADSHOT, AIR ROCKET, SPREE, RAMPAGE, UNSTOPPABLE, GODLIKE, NEW LEADER and HIGH SCORE lines. A quip is never a countdown, objective, round or time call, so it can't be mistaken for one.
- **Your own quips:** put sound files in `voices/<voice>/quips/` (wav, ogg, flac or mp3). They replace that voice's whole quip set. To add to the defaults instead, export them first (the sound editor does this) and put them in the same folder.
