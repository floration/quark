# 1.18.22

## Custom modes
- **Movement settings can be equations.** In a custom mode, any movement setting can be a formula instead of a fixed number. It can depend on other settings (`friction = sqrt(speed)`) or on what you're doing right now: your speed, time in the air or on the ground, height, dash charges, dashing or grappling. Curves with easing (in, out, inout, smooth, expo, back...) and bounds let a setting change smoothly, e.g. `curve(p.hspeed, 2, 12, 2, 0.6, "inout")`. The server and your own prediction both work them out every millisecond, so they play the same online. Make them in the physics editor and save them as a custom mode.
- The HUD's dash pips and hook bar, the grapple onto players, the syringe and bots all follow those equations.
- Modes without equations play exactly as before.

## Compatibility
- Protocol 44: this version can't join servers on 1.18.21 or older, and they can't join it. Old demos still play.

## Physics editor (local tool)
- Compare your settings with 22 other games (Quake, CPMA, Warfork, Xonotic, Half-Life, Counter-Strike, TF2, Titanfall 2, Overwatch and more). Every value has its source.
- Each setting can be fixed, an equation or a curve, with a live plot.
- The Code tab shows the game's real movement code with your values filled in.
- A new Code graph shows which movement step each setting changes.
- The Movement graph gains 3D, locked axis units and A vs B compare.
