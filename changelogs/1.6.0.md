# 1.6.0

- **Joining a full duel:** if the sides are even and full, asking to join calls a vote to let you in. If it passes, you join and the teams go uneven. If the sides are already uneven, you just join the side with room. Ranked stays 1v1.
- **Free for all has no teams:** joining says "joined the game" instead of "joined team A", and bots added to a free-for-all aren't put on a team either.
- **Weapon model in the HUD settings:** a new WEAPON MODEL element. You can move it, push it nearer or further (depth), resize it, give it a custom accent colour, or turn it off with SHOW.
- **SHOW toggle for every HUD element:** health, weapon name, cooldown bars, speed meter and weapon list can each be turned off.
