# 1.19.0

The first production release since 1.17.0. It collects every testing build from 1.18.0 to 1.18.32: lower input and audio latency, cheaper servers, reworked dashes and the hyper, a gameplay feel pass (taps never lost, enemy movement sounds, a death screen, clearer calls), the new Rubik font and a redesigned menu and settings, and custom modes that can change mode rules, weapons and movement. **The network protocol is now 44 (1.17.0 used 42): 1.19.0 doesn't play with 1.17.0 games or servers**, so update both. Dedicated servers update themselves. Your old demos are converted when the game starts, and your settings, maps and files are kept.

## Movement
- **Two dashes, 0.6 s apart, each recharging in 1.2 s.** From full charges you can use both dashes in the air within about 0.72 s. A spent dash recharges for 1.2 s from the end of the dash that used it, then comes back the usual way: landing refills both, a bunny hop one. On the floor you can dash about every 0.72 s.
- **Holding dash dashes as soon as it can**, and a press up to 0.1 s early is kept until the dash is allowed.
- **Dash aim**: looking straight up, 40% of a dash's speed goes up (was 64%); looking straight down, 90% goes down (was 64%).
- **Hyper (super dash)**: it leaves at 5 m/s faster than you were going (at least 11, at most 36), instead of a flat 30. It leaves the floor as if your dash bounced off it: the further down you look, the more of that speed goes up, up to 90% looking straight down. From a run that's about 0.45 m high at 20 degrees down, 1.9 m at 45 degrees and 3.9 m straight down.
- **A dash into a wall bounces off at 16 m/s** (was 24). A dash jump still leaves at 24, and a ground dash aimed a little upward can now be dash-jumped too.
- **Quick dash** (duel QUICK DASH and DASHING BLADES, the BLADES arena): two charges, 0.2 s apart, 0.4 s recharge.
- **A/D air strafing is stronger** (air strafe cap and air acceleration up 10-15%): strafe jumping builds speed faster, about 20.7 m/s after one second from a run instead of 18.2. W-only air turning is 10% slower.
- **Strafe jumping is the same at any frame rate** (at 60 fps you used to lose about 5% speed against a fast machine; now under 0.1%).
- **Short taps always count**: a jump, dash or grapple press shorter than a frame is never lost, and a jump tapped up to 0.1 s before landing jumps the moment you land.
- **Ladders**: only W and S climb (S climbs down); A and D move you sideways.
- **Stairs are smooth**: running up steps no longer jolts the camera.
- **Fewer corrections online**: the syringe refill and grappling an enemy are predicted.
- **Grappling a wall you're standing against** grabs once (at most once a second while held) instead of replaying the grapple sound every frame.

## Weapons and combat
- **Rifle body 5.0** (was 4.95), **shotgun head pellet 2.6** (was 2.55): 20 rifle body shots and two point-blank head blasts now kill.
- **Sniper body shot 40** (was 50). A headshot still does 50: 2 headshots or 3 body shots to kill.
- **Your own rocket splash ramps up smoothly with height**: a rocket at your knees costs about 10 HP instead of 34. Rocket jumps are unchanged. Rocket splash now also hits a player peeking over cover.
- **Weapon switches show instantly**: your weapon, HUD, zoom and shots change the moment you press the key, not one ping later. After a knife or syringe throw you go back to the weapon you held, not the rocket.
- **Sniper, crossbow and rocket keep a click up to 0.1 s early** and fire the moment they're ready.
- **Shots line up better with what you saw**: lag compensation is exact between ticks, and refire is timed by your own commands.
- **Knocking someone off the map within 3 s is your kill** (rocket, crossbow knock, grapple pull).
- **Shotgun HEADSHOT** only when at least half the blast hit the head. The bubble stream no longer gives away enemies behind walls, and a click right after you respawn no longer plays a shot the server never fires.

## Sound
- **You hear enemies move**: their jumps, landings (louder when hard), dashes, supers and hypers, in 3D. Enemy footsteps are heard on stairs and no longer play in mid-air.
- **Distant gunfire**: a hidden shooter within 60 m is heard as a muffled shot from roughly where they are.
- **Sounds behind you are quieter and duller** than in front, and **3D sounds follow you while they play** when you turn.
- **Your hurt sound is calmer**: at most once every 0.15 s, louder for bigger hits.
- **Quips get their own setting**: QUIPS on/off and QUIPS VOLUME. Quips are only hype lines (headshot, sprees, new leader...), never a countdown or objective call. You can put your own in `voices/<voice>/quips/`.
- **EFFECTS VOLUME and ANNOUNCER VOLUME** set the effects and the announcer on their own.
- **Hide and seek and tag: everyone hears the locator** from where the seeker or tagger stands.
- **Windows audio goes through WASAPI**: about 10 ms of buffering (was 30), about 3 ms where the driver allows it, and **EXCLUSIVE AUDIO** for about 3 ms on more devices.
- Three announcer lines that never played (AIRBORNE, TEN SECONDS, FIVE SECONDS) are gone; every voice has 27 lines.

## HUD
- **The death screen** shows who killed you, with what, their HP (not in ranked) and the respawn countdown.
- **A red arc round the crosshair** points to whoever hit you, and **enemy rifle shots draw tracers**.
- **Kills**: a red X kill marker, and a killed player you can see drops with a death sound and a red burst.
- **Instant hit sparks** for your rifle, sniper, shotgun and laser hits, and **your rockets, bolts and knives appear the moment you fire**, from your weapon.
- **The round clock is back** under the mode title in every timed mode (it had been missing since 1.17.0).
- **The dash bar**: a dash you have but can't use yet is a light grey pip that turns blue the moment you can dash; a used dash is empty until it comes back.
- The weapon bar fills over alt-fire refire and the spawn lock. The notices under the score bar are one tidy list.

## Modes
- **Bomb calls**: your job at every round start (ATTACK / DEFEND), halftime's side switch, and a dropped or picked-up bomb.
- **Payload checkpoints** play POINT TAKEN / POINT LOST. **FFA** calls only your own lead changes and shows your place under the mode title ("2ND OF 8").
- **Payload and multi point**: round 1 ends with how far the attackers got ("TEAM A pushed 57 m - TEAM B must beat it"), round 2 ends as soon as the result is settled, and a level attack goes to the side that got there sooner. No more tiebreaker rounds.
- **Railyard**: past the last checkpoint, attackers respawn in 3 s.
- **1v1, duel and tourney**: 2.5 s between rounds (was 4), and the calls use player names.
- **Hide and seek**: a seeker who falls or dies is back after 2 s; ties go to whoever stayed hidden longest, then rock paper scissors; the seeker sees hiders within 24 m without a line-of-sight check, so close hiders don't pop in late.
- **Spawns**: FFA picks a random unseen spawn among the farthest ones; team spawns avoid enemies' sight.
- **Spectating in team modes**: after you die you watch your killer for 1.5 s, then only teammates.
- **An emptied server stops playing** (unranked): when a side or everyone leaves, the match waits 10 s, then gives the side still there a forfeit win or goes back to warmup.
- Fixed: tag players knocked out in one match started the next one already out; the Railyard cart's collision was at the wrong end of the track in team B's attacks; killstreaks carried over between matches.

## Bots
- **Bots path round walls, crates and posts** instead of walking into them (Depot's gatehouse, Summit's west spawn and others).
- **Bomb vs bots on Depot and Lodestone works**: defenders split across the sites.
- **Bots turn toward whoever hurt them**, even a sniper they couldn't hear. **Play vs bots 1V1 is one bot**, and DUEL uses the duel team size.

## Menus and settings
- **A new look** for the menus, settings, in-game pages and map editor: a title logo drawn like the app icon in your team colours, card pickers for maps, modes, rulesets and ladders, and BACK always bottom-left.
- **Settings has six tabs** (PLAYER, GAMEPLAY, CONTROLS, VIDEO + AUDIO, CROSSHAIR, HUD) with hints, colour pickers instead of R / G / B sliders, and right-click to unbind a key.
- **Live HUD preview** on the HUD tab: the HUD stays on screen and the element you edit moves, resizes and recolours as you go.
- **DEVICES**: pick the sound output, the monitor for fullscreen and borderless, and the refresh rate for exclusive fullscreen (the microphone pick is stored for later).
- **Rubik Bold is the new UI font**, and **SETTINGS > FONT** picks another: Work Sans, Quark Classic, look-alikes of game fonts, or any .ttf / .otf you put in `mods/fonts`.
- **HOST > ADVANCED** holds the network options and **BOT BRAIN**. Custom mode sliders show the real value (GRAVITY: 22.0 M/S2), with a new DASH + GRAPPLE tab.
- **MAX FPS is a slider** (right-click: your refresh rate), and the click that brings the game back after alt-tab no longer fires.

## Display, input, performance and netcode
- **Latent Sync** (Windows, the new default PRESENT setting): no tearing, at nearly no-vsync latency. Linux and macOS default to NO VSYNC.
- **Less time from mouse to screen**: the mouse is read again just before the world is drawn, raw mouse input runs on its own thread on Windows, LOW LATENCY ULTRA is paced like NVIDIA Reflex, and windowed vsync uses the driver's vsync (one refresh less delay).
- **F3 shows a mouse-to-photon graph**; F4 shows only that number. VOTE START moved to F5.
- **GPU MAX CLOCKS** (Windows, NVIDIA) keeps the GPU from clocking down mid-frame.
- **Fewer frame-time spikes**: no allocations per frame, about 7x cheaper text, and no more once-a-second stutters.
- **The official maps ship with their lighting baked**: no bake on first join (Midtown 5.3 s to 0.05 s), and cached loads are about 2.5x faster. The download grows by about 12 MB.
- **Kill feed, chat and name events are never lost** when packets arrive out of order or a snapshot is crowded. Remote players are drawn 7-16 ms closer to the present, and your own position is drawn as of now.

## Servers
- **Much less CPU**: a Linux server with players used about 30% of a core on a small cloud VM and now uses 10-12%.
- **The tick rate steps down only on real trouble**: late ticks in two 3-second checks in a row (one short host pause no longer drops it to 500 Hz), heavy load, or bandwidth trouble for most players (the snapshot rate drops first). `status` shows the rates and why they last changed.
- **Dedicated servers with nobody on pause**: no ticks and next to no CPU until a player joins, who starts at warmup.
- New options: `--max-upload-kbps N`, `--tick-spin auto|N` and `--botbrain <name>`.

## Custom modes and modding
- **Mode logic**: a `.qmode` can carry a `logic` section that changes scoring, round ends, respawns, who can hurt whom, timers and announcements. Servers playing it show `[name#hash]` in the server list.
- **Weapon sets**: a custom mode can change any weapon's numbers and add hit and kill rules (`weaponset=<name>`). Joining players get the set automatically.
- **Movement equations**: any movement setting in a custom mode can be a formula or curve (`friction = sqrt(speed)`), worked out the same way on the server and in your prediction.
- **Bot brains**: bots can play `.qbrain` files from your `bots/` folder (`addbot ... brain=<name>`, or HOST > ADVANCED > BOT BRAIN).
- **More models can be replaced** (arms, bomb, payload cart, checkpoint gates), and replacement models are drawn like the built-in ones. Replacement sounds listed in `sound-levels.txt` keep their own loudness.
- All of these are for unranked games: **ranked matches and time trials always play the standard rules, weapons, movement and bots, and show the shipped models**, whatever is in your overrides folder.
- Local editor tools for modders (not in the game download): sound, model, bot, gamemode, weapons and physics editors under `tools/`.

## Fixes
- **STOP HOSTING no longer closes the game** on Windows (and BACK TO EDITOR, ending vs bots or a time trial, or a dropped connection while hosting).
- ALT TOGGLE scope zooms with the toggle; weapon kick recovers the same at any frame rate and while scoped.
- Giant and double-size enemies' shots start at their eyes; spectators see the watched player's laser and sniper shots.
- See-through parts of custom weapon models show in first person; a fresh announcer line is no longer cut off when 64 sounds play.
- Editor: big maps stay smooth while editing, and an online edit can no longer change the wrong object.
