# 1.18.7

A responsiveness, movement and HUD update: weapon switches show instantly, your two dashes can be used 0.6 s apart and recharge in 1.2 s, the wall bounce and super dash speeds change, the round clock is back, and the dash bar is clearer. **1.18.7 changes the network protocol (43): it doesn't play with 1.18.6 or older games and servers**, so update both. Old demos are converted when the game starts.

## Responsiveness
- **Weapon switches show instantly**: your weapon, HUD, zoom and shots change the moment you press the key, instead of one ping later. Holding fire through a switch no longer silences your own shots (the laser's beam and sound used to stop while it kept firing).
- Pressing one weapon key, then another, then the first again within one ping no longer leaves you on the second.

## Movement
- **Dashes are 0.6 s apart and recharge in 1.2 s** (was a 1.2 s cooldown between any two dashes). From full charges in the air you can use both dashes within a second (about 0.72 s apart). A spent dash recharges for 1.2 s from the end of the dash that used it, then comes back the usual way: landing refills both, a bunny hop one. On the floor that means a dash about every 0.72 s.
- A dash jump still gives back the dash it used at once, so from two charges you keep both; a dash that's still recharging from earlier stays out until its 1.2 s.
- **Dash tech speeds**: a dash into a wall now bounces off at **16 m/s** (was 24); a dash jump still leaves at 24; a **super dash** now leaves **5 m/s faster than you were going before the dash** (at least 11, at most 36), instead of a flat 30 (up to 36) however fast you were. Out of a run that's 14.5 m/s (was 30).
- **Quick dash** (duel QUICK DASH / DASHING BLADES, BLADES arena): 0.2 s apart and a 0.4 s recharge (was a 0.4 s cooldown).
- Custom modes: the DASH COOLDOWN setting (standard 0.6 s) scales the recharge too; SUPER DASH BOOST is now the m/s a super dash adds (standard 5, was x1.25); DASH SPEED also scales the wall bounce's 16. Old custom mode files keep working.

## HUD
- **The round clock is back** under the mode title in every timed mode: FFA, tag (its 60 s fuse), hide and seek, bomb (the fuse once it's planted), payload, both-ways payload, multi point and race. It had been missing since 1.17.0.
- **The dash bar**: a dash you have but can't use yet is a whole light grey pip, and it turns blue the moment you can dash. A used dash is empty until it comes back. The pips used to grow from empty, so one dash looked like it had spent both charges.
