# Next (full, testing builds since 1.19.7)

Every change on `testing` since production 1.19.7, with what changed and why. The release PR renames this file to `changelogs/full/<VERSION>.md`. Protocol 68 and map format 14, unchanged.

## Tutorial (1.19.9 "Tutorial update"; q: "show a demo of a bot doing whatever movement tech, then puts you on a map that shows the bot doing it and gives you instructions on how to, all the special moves and combos")

- **What it is.** MAIN MENU > TUTORIAL (or `quark --tutorial [LESSON[:PHASE]]`) hosts a local game like TIME TRIAL (`Game::startTutorial` -> `startHost` with `ServerConfig::tutorial`): the map `tutorial`, FFA, three players (you, the GHOST and, in the two grapple-a-player lessons, the PARTNER), warmup for good (`autoStart` off: no clock, no scores). Each lesson has a **DEMO** and a **TRY** phase:
  - **DEMO:** the camera follows the ghost from behind and over your lane. Your input is held. The ghost's keys light up under your own binds' names (W / A / S / D, JUMP, DASH, GRAPPLE, FIRE, ALT FIRE), with where it aims (level, a little down, straight down, up) and its turning. SUPER / HYPER pop up over it as it does them. JUMP, ENTER or a click hands over to you; otherwise one loop of the script plays.
  - **TRY:** you at your lane's start, the ghost looping beside you. The steps are on the left and the goal has a bar and a number. The lesson's check runs on your own predicted movement every frame, so the bar moves as you see yourself move. Done: LESSON DONE, NEXT: <lesson>, and 2.5 s later the next lesson. After the last: TUTORIAL COMPLETE (LESSONS, MODES GUIDE: INFO > MODES, PLAY VS BOTS).
  - **Keys:** RACE RESET (R) starts the lesson over and the CHAT key (ENTER) skips (the tutorial has nobody to chat to; the console still opens). Esc: RESUME, WATCH AGAIN, SKIP LESSON, START OVER, LESSONS, SETTINGS, LEAVE TUTORIAL, QUIT. LESSONS lists all 28 in four columns (three first, but ten KEY TECH lessons pushed BACK off a 720p screen), the ten KEY TECH ones first under a gold KEY TECH heading (a gold bar on each), and a green mark on each one done (`tutorial_done`, a bit per lesson, kept across sessions; an int whose limit is 2^30, so `static_assert(L_COUNT <= 30)`).
  - **First launch:** the main menu shows WELCOME TO QUARK once, with START TUTORIAL or NOT NOW (Esc). Either answer sets `tutorial_offered=1`. Existing players see it once too, since their config has no such key yet. It isn't shown in the UI test or screenshot runs (`--tutorial-offer` forces it for screenshots).
- **The lessons (one per tech, using the game's own mechanics and names; nothing invented): 28, ten of them KEY TECH.** q: "big techs that are important"; "another key tech is grappling other players, and when you get grappled, dashing out of it / also rocket grapple is considered a key tech"; "find more movement combos for key techs"; the bubble launch: "if it doesnt [kill you] thats cool". In order (the order of the bays and of NEXT):
  - Walking (run and jump two hurdles to a line at 44 m), AD strafing (bunny hops holding one strafe key and turning with it: 12 m/s with no dash or grapple), the dash (an air dash across a 9 m lava gap), sideways dashes (a dash holding only LEFT, one holding only RIGHT), super (2), hyper (2).
  - **Pop** (KEY TECH): a super, then the dash held straight down with jump as you come down: the dash goes as its cooldown ends (930 ms in), a hyper off the floor with the super's 24 m/s, 90% of it up: 26.1 m/s up, 14.2 m high. Goal: 12 m up out of a hyper from 20 m/s or more (`G_POP`).
  - Super chaining (hyper every landing, now aimed 15 degrees down, not 20: more of each hyper stays level, 35 m/s where 20 degrees gave 34; goal 27 m/s), the dash slide (0.8 s), the wall bounce (2).
  - **Ramp dash**: a 60 degree wedge 18 m in. Walking, its face is a wall; a dash into it head on runs on up the face at the dash's 24 m/s: the ghost's dash 1.6 m before it takes it 9.0 m up. Goal: 5 m up past the ramp's foot with a dash since you were on the floor (`G_RAMP`).
  - The grapple (onto a 10 m ledge).
  - **Ceiling hang**: hook the ceiling a few metres ahead and hold: you hang and swing under it. Straight overhead the hook pulls you in and lets go at its 1.2 m release distance, so the text says to hook more than 1 m to the side. Goal: 1.5 s hooked to the map with the anchor 2 m or more above you, off the floor (`G_HANG`).
  - **Swing turn**: a super to 24 m/s, then the pillar 32 m in hooked as it comes up beside you and let go as you pass it: the ghost turns 41 degrees and keeps 24 m/s. Held on, the hook brakes you from 24 to 15 m/s, so the text says to let go. Goal: 35 degrees of heading turned on a hook at 20 m/s or more (`G_SWING`). The ghost aims with `LessonDef::aimPoint` (the pillar), and the demo says AIM: AT THE PILLAR.
  - Grapple walking (16 m/s along the floor).
  - **Hook-start chain**: grapple walk to 24 m/s, let go and hyper every landing aimed 15 degrees down: 29, 34, 35 m/s, 1.3 s after letting go. A hyper above 36 caps you, so 35 is the top, and the text says so. Goal: 34 m/s (`G_SPEED`, no dash or grapple counted since standing).
  - **Grapple a player** and **dash out of a grapple** (KEY TECH, below). Grapple a player has a 4th step: with someone latched, your own dash doesn't break your pull (it follows you, and drags them sideways: 2.5 m/s in the test), so shotgun or knife them as they arrive.
  - Rocket jump.
  - **Fall bounce**: a rocket jump straight up (17 m), jump held all the way down: a landing hop is jumpSpeed + min((impact - jumpSpeed) x 0.5, 8), at most 15.7 m/s, reached from 11.7 m, and it gives a dash charge back. Goal: a 15 m/s hop off a landing with no dash (`G_HOPUP`).
  - **Rocket super** (KEY TECH): a super, then a rocket straight down 0.3 s into it: 33.3 m/s level, past the dash's 32 cap. Goal: 32.5 m/s level thrown by your own blast since leaving the floor (`G_KNOCKSPEED`).
  - **Rocket pop** (KEY TECH): the pop with the rocket fired in the very tick the hyper's dash starts: 47.4 m/s up, 47.6 m high. Goal: 30 m up out of a hyper from 20 m/s (`G_LAUNCH`).
  - **The launch** (KEY TECH, below).
  - **Hook boost** (KEY TECH): grapple walking at 24 m/s, a rocket straight down while still hooked, let go 40 ms later (held on, the hook pulls you back toward it): 40.4 m/s level. Goal: 37 m/s. Outside the tutorial the blast costs about 3 hp.
  - Sticky jump.
  - **Sticky stack** (KEY TECH): three stickies at your feet (each armed 0.35 s after landing), then jump and set them all off: 21.9, 36.2, 50.5 m/s up for one, two, three; 53.2 m high. Goal: 40 m up.
  - **Bubble launch** (KEY TECH, below).
  - **The rocket grapple** (KEY TECH): 0.4 s on your own rocket. It was called rocket riding; renamed to q's name, `--tutorial rocketgrapple`. (Hooking your own rocket, as q named it; the hook boost is a separate lesson.)
- **The two rules, measured and in the lessons' steps** (rocket pop, rocket super, hook boost, hook-start chain): a dash erases a blast's push (a rocket at your feet throws you 17.0 m up; the same with a dash 35 ms after the shot: 0.2 m), so fire after the dash or hyper, never just before one; and a dash above 32 m/s (a hyper above 36) caps you down (the rocket super's 33.3 m/s, then an air dash: 32.0). The rocket pop's timing is strict: the rocket 0 / 10 / 20 ms after the hyper gives 47.4 / 44.3 / 41.1 m/s up, about 3 m/s per 10 ms late, and the text says "10 MS LATE LOSES 3 M/S".
- **The bubble launch, measured** (q: "if it doesnt [kill you] thats cool"). Standing still, aim straight down and alt-fire the bubble gun: the whole stream (25 bubbles with no enemy within 30 m, `bubbleStreamCount`) pops under you: **58.4 m/s up, 72.8 m high**, every 1.8 s (the stream's reload). Out of the tutorial it costs 11.9 hp. A jump first: 48.0 m/s up (rising already, the slower bubbles can't catch you, so the text says "from standing or a slow rise only"). From a run with a jump: 42.6 m/s up and 25.3 m/s along. With an enemy within 5 m the stream is 5 bubbles: 16.6 m/s up (enemy 3 m off), and the text says so. Its bay is 30 x 80. Goal: 55 m up, thrown by your own bubbles (`G_KNOCKUP`). **One server change for it:** in the tutorial nobody counts as an enemy for the stream's size (`cfg_.tutorial`: the ghost in the next lane, 12 m off, would cut your stream to 11 bubbles, and you the ghost's); the test puts a bot in your lane and checks the full 58.4 m/s. Nothing changes outside the tutorial.
- **The launch, measured** (quark_tests tutorial: the ghost's script on the real server and map). Grapple walk with the hook just under level until 24 m/s, then in one tick: release, pitch -89, JUMP + DASH + FIRE. In pmove the hyper comes first: carried speed 24 + 5 = 29 m/s, mostly up because the dash aims down (up share 0.9). The rocket goes off at your feet about 33 ms later and adds about 22 m/s up. Result: **48.7 m/s up and 17.2 m/s level off the floor, 50 m high, 65.7 m along.** The same input with the rocket held back (the hyper alone) gives 26.1 m/s up and 14.2 m high, so the rocket more than triples the height. The order matters: if the blast came first it would lift you off the floor and there would be no floor for the hyper.
- **Grappling players, measured** (quark_tests tutorial: the real latch on the real server; no new mechanics, the tutorial only lets this one pair hook). Grapple a player: the ghost's hook latched onto the PARTNER running across the bay at 9.5 m/s, the two pulled together up to 24 m/s, and let go as they met 0.83 s later (meeting at the release distance). Dash out of a grapple: the PARTNER's hook caught the ghost at 601 ms; pulled toward it at 13.3 m/s, the ghost dashed 350 ms later and was free **the same tick** (pmove's dash clears `MF_PULLED`, `syncLatch` ends the latch, the PARTNER's hook goes to its 1 s cooldown). As you, with no dash you're pulled up to 24 m/s over 14 m for as long as it holds its grapple (0.9 s here; at most 3 s, `kGrappleLatchMax`); a dash 300 ms in breaks free on that tick. Grabbing something with your own grapple breaks free too (pmove, the same rule), and the lesson says so.
- **The PARTNER** (the two grapple-a-player lessons, `LessonDef::partner`): a second scripted bot, spectating in every other lesson. Its script loops with the ghost's and it's put back at its start every loop. It runs across the middle of the bay 30 m in (grapple a player), or stands 34 m in and hooks the ghost in the demo and you in your turn (`tutorial try`, sent when your turn starts; dash out of a grapple). A script step with pitch `kAimAt` aims at the other one: the server works out the yaw and pitch every tick (a bot's view time is now, so no rewind). Only the lesson's pair may hook (`Server::tutorialHookAllowed`, through `nativeCanDamage`, which in the tutorial decides only grapples: `damagePlayer` stops all else): anyone onto the PARTNER, or the PARTNER onto anyone. The client's grapple prediction catches only the PARTNER, and only in its lesson (`NetClient::hookPlayers`, a bit per player, replacing the earlier all-or-nothing flag).
- **The ghost** (src/game/tutorial_server.cpp) is a server bot (`addBot`, named GHOST) whose `BotBrain::script` returns the lesson's input script (`tut::ghostCmd`): `Seg`s of held keys, a yaw rate and a pitch, run through the normal pmove and weapons. That gives real rockets, stickies and rocket riding, drawn by the normal player and projectile rendering, for no new wire messages. Each loop it's put back at its start (fresh MoveState, its projectiles cleared).
  - The game sends `tutorial <lesson> <epoch ms>` (a string command, no protocol change). The epoch is the view time + 0.5 s. The keys shown are the script's at the drawn time minus the epoch, so they match what the ghost is doing on screen. The server clamps the epoch to between 1 s ago and 3 s ahead. `tutorial reset` sends you back to the start.
  - Spawns: `tutorialSpawnAt` puts you at your lane's start and the ghost at its own.
  - Nobody touches anybody: `nativeCanHit` is false between players, `damagePlayer` returns early, projectiles skip player boxes (`tickProjectiles`), and the grapple catches no player but the PARTNER in its lessons (above). Your own blasts still throw you, with no damage. `grappleTargets` lets this one bot ride rockets (bots never do otherwise).
  - Client side, the ghost counts as friendly (your colour, no hit tests against it), and the score box reads TUTORIAL / LESSON n OF 28 instead of the warmup line.
- **The check** (`tut::Check`, src/game/tutorial.h): the lesson's goal, fed the movement and its pmove events (ME_*) once a frame. It's a few comparisons with no allocation. Standing still on the floor for 0.3 s starts a new try (so a strafe speed after a dash doesn't count). A blast is told from a jump by the rise in upward speed with the jump's own speed taken off, no dash and not hooked.
- **The map `tutorial`** (tools/maps/tutorial.py): 28 sealed 24 m-wide bays side by side along z, each running along +x from x -90 and built for its one tech. 12 to 80 m tall (72 m for the launch hall, 80 m for the bubble launch, 64 m for the sticky stack, 60 m for the rocket pop) and 12 to 180 m long. The ramp dash bay has a 60 degree wedge onto a 10.4 m shelf, the swing turn bay a floor-to-ceiling pillar 32 m in. The dash bay has a lava pit with striped edges, the bounce bay a striped end wall, and the grapple / jump bays ledges with flat faces (a 5 cm overhang snagged the grapple). Tall bays have rows of lights and glow bands up their walls.
  - **Side by side, not in a row:** the first layout was a 1137 m row along x, and the ghost was drawn stuck at x 512, because snapshots carry positions as int16 at 1/64 m (`Buffer::vecq`, +-512 m). The new layout fits in x -91..91, z -351..351.
  - It's hidden from every map list, vote and picker by name (`mapHiddenFromLists`): the client's map list, the server's map rotation, and the dedicated server's fallback pick. Only the tutorial hosts it.
- **Performance.** When the tutorial isn't running, the cost is a few `tut_.on` tests. While it runs, the text is formatted once a lesson and again when the pause menu closes (the binds may have changed). The readout is written into a string that keeps its capacity, the key names are cached, and nothing is allocated per frame. The demo camera turns off the late latch (no mouse re-read for a view that isn't yours) and hides the first-person models and your own HUD.
- **Translations.** 211 new strings, in all 9 languages (the bot names GHOST and PARTNER stay as they are, as on their nametags). Step text is drawn as "KEYS: TEXT", so each side of ": " is translated (your key names stay as they are). The ja / ko / zh-CN atlases were re-baked for 66 new characters.
- **Tests.** `quark_tests tutorial`:
  - The lesson table, scripts, the map (every start has floor and room, every bay's far wall where its length says), and the check on made-up movement.
  - Every lesson's ghost on the real server and map, fed into the very check you get. Each one passes within one loop, and the launch is also run without its rocket to prove the rocket is what makes it. The combos' numbers above are asserted (within a tolerance), so a change to the movement that moves them fails the test and the lesson texts get updated with it.
  - The combos' variants and rules (`testTutorialCombos`): sticky stacks of 1 / 2 / 3, the rocket pop's timing, a dash erasing a blast, the air dash's 32 cap, and the bubble launch standing / after a jump / from a run / with you in your lane / with an enemy 3 m off / its cost.
  - The PARTNER's lessons with the real latch: who may hook whom in each lesson (and nobody in the others), the ghost latched onto the running PARTNER with no damage, the PARTNER's hook on the ghost broken by the dash on the dash's tick with the hook's cooldown after, and a bot standing in for you: held without a dash, free on the tick of a dash 300 ms in, and the lesson's check done only then.
  - `TUT_TRACE=<lesson>` prints a trace of it.
  - The map passes the `maps`, `mapbounds`, `mapbounds2` and `bounds` groups.

## Info screen

INFO > CONTROLS had three lines left over from older movement and weapon numbers (each checked against the code, and re-translated in all 9 languages):
- **Air drift.** It said holding a direction in the air "drifts you up to run speed". The drift is 0 since protocol 63 (owner, 2026-11-01: "change air drift to 0"; `kAirDriftAccel`). What's left is the air acceleration's 2.3 m/s cap, so now: you keep your speed in the air, a held direction steers it, and a standing jump drifts 2.3 m/s at most.
- **Dash exit speed.** It said every dash leaves you 1 m/s faster. That's only on the floor; in the air the dash adds max(1, 0.35 x (32 - your speed)) (`kDashAirGap`, owner 2026-10-28), so now: "on the floor by 1 m/s, in the air by about a third of what you are below 32 m/s (from 10 m/s you leave at 17.7)", and that a dash above 32 slows you to 32. ("About a third", not "35%": the language extractor reads "% o" as a format.)
- **Syringe.** It said 10 hp a use and +30 hp thrown; they were halved to 5 and 15 (owner, 2026-10-31: "half syringe healing").
