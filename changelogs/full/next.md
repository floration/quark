# Next (full, testing builds since 1.19.7)

Every change on `testing` since production 1.19.7, with what changed and why. The release PR renames this file to `changelogs/full/<VERSION>.md`. Protocol 68 and map format 14, unchanged.

## Tutorial (1.19.9 "Tutorial update"; q: "show a demo of a bot doing whatever movement tech, then puts you on a map that shows the bot doing it and gives you instructions on how to, all the special moves and combos")

- **What it is.** MAIN MENU > TUTORIAL (or `quark --tutorial [LESSON[:PHASE]]`) hosts a local game like TIME TRIAL (`Game::startTutorial` -> `startHost` with `ServerConfig::tutorial`): the map `tutorial`, FFA, two players (you and the GHOST), warmup for good (`autoStart` off: no clock, no scores). Each lesson has a **DEMO** and a **TRY** phase:
  - **DEMO:** the camera follows the ghost from behind and over your lane. Your input is held. The ghost's keys light up under your own binds' names (W / A / S / D, JUMP, DASH, GRAPPLE, FIRE, ALT FIRE), with where it aims (level, a little down, straight down, up) and its turning. SUPER / HYPER pop up over it as it does them. JUMP, ENTER or a click hands over to you; otherwise one loop of the script plays.
  - **TRY:** you at your lane's start, the ghost looping beside you. The steps are on the left and the goal has a bar and a number. The lesson's check runs on your own predicted movement every frame, so the bar moves as you see yourself move. Done: LESSON DONE, NEXT: <lesson>, and 2.5 s later the next lesson. After the last: TUTORIAL COMPLETE (LESSONS, MODES GUIDE: INFO > MODES, PLAY VS BOTS).
  - **Keys:** RACE RESET (R) starts the lesson over and the CHAT key (ENTER) skips (the tutorial has nobody to chat to; the console still opens). Esc: RESUME, WATCH AGAIN, SKIP LESSON, START OVER, LESSONS, SETTINGS, LEAVE TUTORIAL, QUIT. LESSONS lists all 15, the KEY TECH first on its own row with a gold bar, and a green mark on each one done (`tutorial_done`, a bit per lesson, kept across sessions).
  - **First launch:** the main menu shows WELCOME TO QUARK once, with START TUTORIAL or NOT NOW (Esc). Either answer sets `tutorial_offered=1`. Existing players see it once too, since their config has no such key yet. It isn't shown in the UI test or screenshot runs (`--tutorial-offer` forces it for screenshots).
- **The lessons (one per tech, using the game's own mechanics and names; nothing invented).** Walking (run and jump two hurdles to a line at 44 m), AD strafing (bunny hops holding one strafe key and turning with it: 12 m/s with no dash or grapple), the dash (an air dash across a 9 m lava gap), sideways dashes (a dash holding only LEFT, one holding only RIGHT), super (jump during a floor dash: 2 of them), hyper (dash down into the floor holding jump: 2), super chaining (hyper on every landing: 27 m/s), the dash slide (dash down into the floor without jumping, after a super: 0.8 s of ice grip), the wall bounce (2 head-on dash bounces off the striped end wall), the grapple (onto a 10 m ledge), grapple walking (hook the floor far ahead and get dragged at 16 m/s), rocket jump and sticky jump (7 m up onto an 8 m ledge), rocket riding (0.4 s on your own rocket), and **the launch, marked KEY TECH** (q: "grapple to 24m/s, rocket straight down, hyper straight down; you go flying cheaply and quickly").
- **The launch, measured** (quark_tests tutorial: the ghost's script on the real server and map). Grapple walk with the hook just under level until 24 m/s, then in one tick: release, pitch -89, JUMP + DASH + FIRE. In pmove the hyper comes first: carried speed 24 + 5 = 29 m/s, mostly up because the dash aims down (up share 0.9). The rocket goes off at your feet about 33 ms later and adds about 22 m/s up. Result: **48.7 m/s up and 17.2 m/s level off the floor, 50 m high, 65.7 m along.** The same input with the rocket held back (the hyper alone) gives 26.1 m/s up and 14.2 m high, so the rocket more than triples the height. The order matters: if the blast came first it would lift you off the floor and there would be no floor for the hyper.
- **The ghost** (src/game/tutorial_server.cpp) is a server bot (`addBot`, named GHOST) whose `BotBrain::script` returns the lesson's input script (`tut::ghostCmd`): `Seg`s of held keys, a yaw rate and a pitch, run through the normal pmove and weapons. That gives real rockets, stickies and rocket riding, drawn by the normal player and projectile rendering, for no new wire messages. Each loop it's put back at its start (fresh MoveState, its projectiles cleared).
  - The game sends `tutorial <lesson> <epoch ms>` (a string command, no protocol change). The epoch is the view time + 0.5 s. The keys shown are the script's at the drawn time minus the epoch, so they match what the ghost is doing on screen. The server clamps the epoch to between 1 s ago and 3 s ahead. `tutorial reset` sends you back to the start.
  - Spawns: `tutorialSpawnAt` puts you at your lane's start and the ghost at its own.
  - Nobody touches anybody: `nativeCanDamage` / `nativeCanHit` are false between players, `damagePlayer` returns early, projectiles skip player boxes (`tickProjectiles`), and the grapple catches no player (server: the damage rule; client: `NetClient::noPlayerHooks`). Your own blasts still throw you, with no damage. `grappleTargets` lets this one bot ride rockets (bots never do otherwise).
  - Client side, the ghost counts as friendly (your colour, no hit tests against it), and the score box reads TUTORIAL / LESSON n OF 15 instead of the warmup line.
- **The check** (`tut::Check`, src/game/tutorial.h): the lesson's goal, fed the movement and its pmove events (ME_*) once a frame. It's a few comparisons with no allocation. Standing still on the floor for 0.3 s starts a new try (so a strafe speed after a dash doesn't count). A blast is told from a jump by the rise in upward speed with the jump's own speed taken off, no dash and not hooked.
- **The map `tutorial`** (tools/maps/tutorial.py): 15 sealed 24 m-wide bays side by side along z, each running along +x from x -90 and built for its one tech. They are 12 m tall, with 16 m for the chain and rocket ride, 18 m for the grapple, 24 m for the rocket and sticky jumps and 72 m for the launch hall; 12 to 180 m long. The dash bay has a lava pit with striped edges, the bounce bay a striped end wall, and the grapple / jump bays ledges with flat faces (a 5 cm overhang snagged the grapple). Tall bays have rows of lights and glow bands up their walls.
  - **Side by side, not in a row:** the first layout was a 1137 m row along x, and the ghost was drawn stuck at x 512, because snapshots carry positions as int16 at 1/64 m (`Buffer::vecq`, +-512 m). The new layout fits in x -91..91, z -188..188.
  - It's hidden from every map list, vote and picker by name (`mapHiddenFromLists`): the client's map list, the server's map rotation, and the dedicated server's fallback pick. Only the tutorial hosts it.
- **Performance.** When the tutorial isn't running, the cost is a few `tut_.on` tests. While it runs, the text is formatted once a lesson and again when the pause menu closes (the binds may have changed). The readout is written into a string that keeps its capacity, the key names are cached, and nothing is allocated per frame. The demo camera turns off the late latch (no mouse re-read for a view that isn't yours) and hides the first-person models and your own HUD.
- **Translations.** 125 new strings, in all 9 languages. Step text is drawn as "KEYS: TEXT", so each side of ": " is translated (your key names stay as they are). The ja / ko / zh-CN atlases were re-baked for 32 new characters.
- **Tests.** `quark_tests tutorial`:
  - The lesson table, scripts, the map (every start has floor and room, every bay's far wall where its length says), and the check on made-up movement.
  - Every lesson's ghost on the real server and map, fed into the very check you get. Each one passes within one loop, and the launch is also run without its rocket to prove the rocket is what makes it.
  - `TUT_TRACE=<lesson>` prints a trace of it.
  - The map passes the `maps`, `mapbounds`, `mapbounds2` and `bounds` groups.
