# Next (full, testing builds since 1.19.3)

Every change on `testing` since production 1.19.3, with what changed and why. The release PR renames this file to `changelogs/full/<VERSION>.md`.

## Settings: batch 1, the 4s (owner, 2026-10-30: "go ahead and start working on implementing all of the settings ... score all the setting out of 4"; q ticked all 231 on the scored list)

- **MORE pages.** CONTROLS and VIDEO + AUDIO were full (the VIDEO page's comments already say a row more pushes SOUND into DEVICES), so each gets a MORE page (footer middle, `moreOpen_`, screen paths `/controls/more`, `/video/more`, `--settingstab controls/more`). The rest of 1.19.4's settings go on pages like these instead of squeezing the tabs.
- **Mouse (#4).** `sens_y` (vertical as a multiple of SENSITIVITY), `invert_y`, `zoom_sens` (a multiple of the existing "same feel" zoom scaling, so x1.00 is today's behaviour). Applied in `Game::turn`.
- **Scoreboard toggle (#67).** `score_toggle`: the key flips `scoresOn_` instead of being held.
- **Background cap and mute (#9).** `bg_fps` (default 60, 0 = FPS LIMIT as usual) caps `Game::maxFps()` while the window isn't focused or is minimized (new iconify callback); `bg_mute` sets the master volume to 0 then. Why: an alt-tabbed game running uncapped burns power and heat for nothing; focused play is untouched.
- **Window (#119, #1, #166).** The windowed size and position are saved on every move / resize (not while maximized or fullscreen) and restored at launch if the title bar lands on a monitor that's still there; WINDOW SIZE offers 720p up to 4K that fit the monitor. A display change (DISPLAY or WINDOW SIZE) shows KEEP / REVERT with a 10 s count and reverts by itself (not in the UI test, whose clicks would race the count).
- **Dedicated GPU (#113).** `NvOptimusEnablement` and `AmdPowerXpressRequestHighPerformance` exported from the Windows game, so hybrid laptops use the dedicated GPU; VIDEO > MORE shows GL_RENDERER and hints when it looks integrated.
- **Shadow quality (#3).** `Renderer::setShadowRes` reallocates the shadow map in place (1024 / 2048 / 4096, clamped to the GPU's limit) and updates both programs' texel size; it used to be cfg-only and read at startup.
- **HUD size (#7).** `hud_size` multiplies every HUD element's own SIZE (each element's `k` and `hudFont`). The menus already scale with the window height, so no separate menu scale was added.
- **Crosshair (#13).** `ch_dot` (0 = THICKNESS), `ch_opacity`, `ch_outline_w`, `ch_outline_a`, `ch_wstyle_<weapon>` (a weapon's own style, -1 = the normal one), and COPY / PASTE CODE: the ch_ lines as one `quark-crosshair:` line; pasting reads only ch_ keys, so a code can't change anything else.
- **Hitsounds (#14, #152).** `hit_volume` (0-2), `hit_pack` (-1 = your SOUNDS pack), `headshot_sound` (a higher ding over the hit on a headshot), `kill_sound` (was tied to HITSOUND).
- **Server list (#78).** `browser_sort`, `browser_hide_full`, `browser_hide_empty`, `browser_max_ping`, `fav_servers` (right-click a row; favourites are listed first and never filtered out), `recent_servers` (the last 8 joined; RECENT cycles them into the address field). The list shows 5 rows instead of 6 to make room for the filter row.
- **Race reset (owner, 2026-10-30: "keybind R should restart the map/reset to checkpoint in race/surf modes").** New action RACE RESET (R; bind_version 4 leaves it unbound if R was already taken) sends `racereset`; the server respawns you at your last checkpoint, or with `racereset full` (a second press within 0.5 s, or no checkpoint yet) at the start with the checkpoint count and splits cleared. Nothing new on the wire: it's a player command and the existing EV_SPAWN.

## Settings: batch 2, the 3s (first half)

- **Pages.** `moreOpen_` is now a page number: GAMEPLAY > MORE (1 page), CONTROLS > MORE (1), VIDEO + AUDIO > MORE (3, the footer button reads MORE 2 / MORE 3). Screen paths `/gameplay/more`, `/video/more2`, `/video/more3`; CONTROLS gets a third keys sub-page, MORE KEYS (`/controls/keys2`), because the other two key pages have no free rows.
- **Render scale 25-400% (#250).** Was 50-200%. The renderer already capped the target at the GPU's largest texture, so 400% of a 4K window is clamped to what the driver allows rather than failing.
- **Fog (#43).** `fog`: a multiple of the old fixed density 0.0035 (0 = none), set in both the world and model programs (`Renderer::setFog`; the world program keeps it across map changes).
- **Gamma / contrast / saturation (#29).** `gamma`, `contrast`, `saturation`, a `grade()` at the end of the world, model and sky shaders (after tonemapping and gamma encoding), so the HUD and menus aren't touched. At the default (1, 1, 1) it's one uniform compare per pixel and returns unchanged. Why in those shaders: there's no full-screen post pass at render scale 100% with AA off, and adding one would cost a full-screen draw every frame.
- **Video presets (#56).** `video_preset` cycle: LOW (no player shadows, 1024 shadows, no AA, 75% scale, effects 0.6), MEDIUM (shadows 2048, SMOOTH AA), HIGH (4096, MSAA 4x), COMPETITIVE (no shadows, no AA, no fog, effects 0.5, no kill bursts or recoil kick). It sets those settings once; anything changed afterwards stays.
- **Effects (#30, #49).** `fx_opacity` scales every explosion / spark / burst box's alpha (0 = none drawn), `kill_bursts`, `tracers`, `view_recoil` (the gun model's kick; also moves the muzzle point, so shots still leave the gun you see). Muzzle flash and impact sparks were already switches.
- **Colourblind presets (#11).** Two more TEAM COLORS presets: RED-GREEN (blue / yellow-orange) and BLUE-YELLOW (cyan / red). `colorPairClash` simulates protan, deutan and tritan vision (Machado et al. 2009, full severity, linear RGB) and the PLAYER tab warns when the pair's simulated colours are under 0.4 apart (out of 1.73). Checked: cyan / red, blue / orange and both new presets pass; red / green fails for deutans (0.37), white / yellow for tritans (0.29).
- **Enemy model (#15).** `enemy_model` draws every non-friendly player as that model (client only; ranked already forces STANDARD for everyone and is left alone).
- **Sound groups (#33, #174).** `vol_steps` (your footsteps, jumps, landings), `vol_others_steps` (0-2, everyone else's), `vol_weapons` (every gunshot sound), `vol_objective`: the planted bomb now beeps from where it is, in step with the model's blink (every bomb timer / 20 s, at least 0.15 s), reusing the sticky beep a fifth higher. No UI sounds exist yet, so there's no UI volume.
- **Kill feed / chat (#22).** `feed_time` (6 s), `feed_lines` (6), `feed_mine`, `chat_time` (10 s), `chat_lines` (8), `chat_opacity` (not while typing). Chat history (#138): typing shows 12 lines of the 30 kept, scrolled by the wheel or Page Up / Down.
- **Team chat (#41).** New player command `sayteam`: in a team mode the server sends the EV_CHAT only to the sender's team, with `b = 1` so it shows "(TEAM)"; elsewhere it's an ordinary say. Old clients just show such a line without the tag.
- **Keys (#41, #137, #148, #178).** New actions TEAM CHAT, LAST WEAPON (the weapon held before the current one), SPEC NEXT / SPEC PREV (dead or spectating; a click still cycles), HIDE HUD (`hide_hud_crosshair` keeps the crosshair; the scoreboard still works) - all unbound by default. A bind that takes a key from another action shows "<KEY> WAS ON <ACTION>: UNBOUND THERE" with UNDO for 8 s. The bomb objective line and the F3 hints name your bound key (`Game::keyOf`). INFO's key list said F4 for VOTE START, which has been F5 since bind version 3. `testHeld_` (the UI test's held keys) was 32 bits wide while actions passed 32: now 64, with a static_assert. Ready and inspect have no game system behind them yet, so they're not here.
- **Screen (#28, #60, #176, #25).** `show_fps` / `show_ping` (top right, frames counted over 0.5 s; hidden while F3 is up), `captions` (each announcer line as text under the crosshair for 2 s, through one `Game::announce`), `hp_scaled` (the health bar against the most health seen this life, so 200-health customs and 50-health ones fill correctly; the client isn't told the mode's max, so its spawn health stands in), `ch_head_color` (a headshot's crosshair flash).
- **Controls (#18, #73, #5).** `grapple_toggle` (press to hook, press again to let go; reset on death), `scroll_wrap` / `scroll_skip` (a bit per weapon; number keys still select them), `left_handed` (both first-person models mirror sides and their tilt). Dash stays a tap (a toggle makes no sense for an impulse); CROUCH was already off every key page. A separate viewmodel FOV isn't done: the models are drawn in the world pass with the scene's projection, and their SIZE / DEPTH settings already do what a viewmodel FOV is used for.
- **Display (#218, #121, #112).** `vrr`: swap interval 1 and `maxFps()` capped at refresh - 3 (from the monitor's mode, or DEVICES' REFRESH RATE in fullscreen), the usual G-SYNC / FreeSync recipe that keeps the frame out of vsync's queue. `auto_iconify` (GLFW_AUTO_ICONIFY). `cpu_priority`: PRIORITIZE GAME can ask for HIGH_PRIORITY_CLASS instead of ABOVE_NORMAL (`setGamePriority(level)`).
- **Menus (#161, #165, #193, #77).** `remember_tab`; `confirm_leave` (QUIT, DISCONNECT, STOP HOSTING arm on the first click as "SURE? ..." and act on a second within 3 s); `auto_reconnect` (a "connection to server lost" drop goes back to the last address after 2 s, up to 3 tries, not for a kick, a refusal, a demo or your own server) and RECONNECT next to JOIN GAME; `queue_flash` (a filled queue also plays the finish chime and flashes the taskbar if the game isn't focused).
- **Editor (#195, #196).** Redo: each undo step now keeps its own op beside its inverse (`edUndoFwd_`), so undo moves the step to `edRedo_` and Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z does it again; a new edit clears the redo list; drags and coalesced nudges redo to where they ended. Autosave: every 60 s with unsaved changes the map goes to `editor-autosave.qmap` in the data folder (outside `maps/`, so it never shows in map lists); SAVE removes it; RESTORE AUTOSAVE appears in the panel when one is left from before.

## Settings: batch 2, the 3s (second half)

- **Settings search (#132).** `tools/settings_index.py` reads game.cpp's settings draw functions at build time (a CMake step, like the embedded maps) and writes `settings_index.h`: each widget label written as a string literal, and the tab / MORE page / keys sub-page it's on, so the index can't go stale when a setting moves. The SEARCH field (top right of the settings panel) matches labels and key action names. While the field has focus, up to 14 matches replace the page body (an overlay over the page would sit on top of live widgets); a click on one opens its page, and a click anywhere else closes the list and goes to the page. Only this field's focus counts (`Ui::focused(id)`), not `typing()`, which a slider or colour being typed into also sets.
- **Third bind slot (#140).** `kBindSlots = 3`. Binds are saved as three values per action; older games read the first two and ignore the third, so a config still works if you go back a version. Key rows are now three 82 px slots.
- **Console binds and aliases (#141, #142).** `bind <key> <command>` (`cbind_<code>` in the config), `unbind`, `binds`, `alias <name> <commands>` (`;`-separated, `alias_<name>`), `unalias`, `exec <file>` (from the data folder) and `toggle <cvar>`. A command bind runs on key press, after the action binds. `autoexec.cfg` runs once at start (not in the UI test, which must start from defaults).
- **All settings export / import (#167).** EXPORT puts the config as text (`quark-settings:` then `key=value` lines, keys included, your name / Discord / servers left out) on the clipboard; IMPORT applies every line it recognises and says how many. RESET ALL arms on the first click (SURE?) and keeps name, Discord name and server lists.
- **Demos (#143, #144, #145).** The DEMOS page sorts by date / name / size / map (`demo_sort`), shows sizes, and DELETE asks first. `auto_record`: OFF / EVERY MATCH / RANKED, started on entering the match. `demo_keep` (0 = all) deletes the oldest demos past N when an auto recording starts (never the one being written).
- **Sensitivity converter (#9).** Quark's yaw is 0.022 degrees per count x sensitivity, the same as Source and Quake, so CS2 / Quake / Apex / TF2 carry over 1:1; Valorant is 0.07 and Overwatch 2 / Call of Duty 0.0066. USE <value> sets yours.
- **Fullscreen resolution (#113).** `fs_width` / `fs_height` from `glfwGetVideoModes` (0 = the desktop mode). Applied when fullscreen is (re)entered.
- **Practice (#148).** Admin command `practice savepos|loadpos|god|passive|reset` on your own server, refused in ranked or when another human is connected (so it can't be used against anyone). loadpos sets position, velocity and view and sends EV_SPAWN so prediction snaps there instead of smoothing; passive strips the bots' fire buttons; god makes `canHurt` false for you.
- **Players menu (#156).** Pause > HOST > PLAYERS: everyone on your server with KICK (not yourself), plus ADD BOT, KICK BOTS and RESTART, through the existing admin commands.
- **Race ghost (#179).** Your position every 50 ms on race maps; a finished run faster than the saved one is written to `ghosts/<map>.txt` and drawn as a see-through player along the run, by the time since your start. Capped at 20 minutes of points. Client only.
- **Team panel (#17).** `team_panel`: teammates' names and health bars on the left edge in team modes, and a square per enemy, lit while they're alive (off by default).
- **Match summary (#180).** `match_summary`: your shots, hits, accuracy, damage dealt and best streak for the match under the end-of-match scoreboard, counted on the client.
- **Not in this batch** (still 3s, next): a turning 3D model preview (#128), moving the objective / notice / scoreboard boxes (#21), renaming demos (#139). Viewmodel FOV: see Controls above.

## Settings: batch 3, the 2s (HUD, social, controls, menus, host, editor, launcher)

Worker 2's half of the 2-scored items. Batch-3 keys are table-driven (`b3Keys` in config.cpp: type, bounds, default), parsed by `setBatch3` before the old keys and written by `batch3Text`, so each new key is one line and always clamped. Three MORE pages are new (GAMEPLAY > MORE 2 / 3 / 4, CONTROLS > MORE 2, PLAYER > MORE), each with its screen path for the UI test and its page function in `tools/settings_index.py` so the settings search finds every label. Nothing new runs per frame at the defaults: every feature sits behind its setting's check (a compare), and the three that track state from frame to frame (health, alerts, auto-hide) are a few compares each.

**Feedback (GAMEPLAY > MORE 2)**
- **Damage numbers (#16).** `dmg_num_color` (a normal hit; headshots stay red and big hits gold so the meaning stays), `dmg_num_stack` (quick hits add up / a number a hit / one running total a target until it fades) and `dmg_num_time` (0.3 to 3 s, 0.9 as before).
- **Damage arcs (#23).** `dmg_arcs`, `dmg_arc_color`, `dmg_arc_size` (x0.5 to x2) and `dmg_arc_time`: the arc pointing where a hit you took came from.
- **Kill mark and hit flash (#24).** `kill_mark`, `kill_mark_time`, `kill_mark_color`; `hit_flash_time` (its colours stay on the CROSSHAIR tab, which is the other worker's).
- **Kill text (#153).** `kill_text`: "KILLED <NAME>" over the crosshair for 1.5 s.
- **Low health (#62, #92).** `low_hp` (the share of the mode's max health, so vampire and instagib work too), `low_hp_color`, `low_hp_pulse` and `low_hp_sound` (one beep on crossing the threshold, from `trackHealth`, never repeated while you stay low).
- **Heal numbers (#177).** `heal_nums`: a green +N over your health when it rises (vampire, regen, the syringe); heals within a second add up.
- **Weapon kick (#55).** `recoil_amount` x0 to x2 multiplies the view model's kick (and the muzzle point that follows it); FOV and hand were #5.
- **Per-weapon model (#144).** `wpn_vm_<n>=<shown>,<x>,<y>,<depth>` (written only when not the default): a weapon's model hidden, or moved on top of the HUD tab's WEAPON MODEL element (same units: 720p pixels and mm, -200 to 200). `weaponFrame` takes the weapon, so the muzzle (tracers, beams, your projectiles' start) follows the moved model; hiding one weapon's model also hides its muzzle flash and, with YOUR SHOTS: WITH WEAPON MODEL, its tracers, as the whole-model switch does.

**HUD info (GAMEPLAY > MORE 3)**
- **Speed meter (#69, #200, #199).** `speed_unit` (m/s, the game's own; km/h; ups at 39.37 a metre, as Quake counts), `speed_scale` (the bar's full scale) and `speed_ticks`; `bar_caps` turns each bar's caption on or off.
- **Clock (#69, #158).** `clock_12h` for the verbose TIME line; `clock_mode` (time left / elapsed) and `clock_tenths` (tenths in the last 10 s) for the round clock, drawn after the live line so hudclock.h stays as it is.
- **Verbose lines (#27).** `verbose_lines`: a bit per line of the verbose info block.
- **Auto-hide (#159).** `autohide_health` always / after a change (3 s) and when low / only when low; `autohide_slots` always / after a switch; `autohide_bars` always / while one is recharging.
- **Objective markers (#68).** `obj_size`, `obj_opacity`, `obj_distance` (the distance under the label) and `obj_arrow` (an arrow at the screen edge when the objective is off screen).
- **Scope (#200).** `scope_size`, `scope_color`, `scope_line` (the reticle's width).
- **Network warnings (#71).** `net_icons`: STALLED (nothing received for 0.3 s), LOSS (3% snapshot loss or more) and PING (150 ms or more) badges. `NetClient::lastRecvTime()` was added for the stall; not on your own server or in a demo.
- **Spectator HUD and feed (#202).** `spec_hud` full / minimal; `feed_headshot` adds (HS) to headshot kills in the feed.
- **Stalemate warning (#175).** `drain_warn`: the round modes' last 10 s before health starts draining, over the crosshair, with a beep at 10 and 5.

**Social (GAMEPLAY > MORE 4)**
- **Chat (#36, #98, #168).** `team_chat_only`, `chat_filter` (a built-in word list, matched case-blind at a word's start, the rest of the word starred), `chat_one_color` + `chat_color`, `sys_color`, `chat_box` + `chat_box_opacity` (a box behind the chat lines), `chat_stamps` (the local time before each line), `mention_hl` (a line naming you in gold) and `mention_sound`.
- **System messages (#242).** `sys_msgs` all / important (votes, kicks, ranked, errors) / off.
- **Name colours (#70).** `other_name_colors` off draws everyone else's name white.
- **Streamer mode (#79).** `streamer`: server addresses, invite codes and the Discord code shown as stars, other players' names as PLAYER 1, 2..., the data folder path hidden.
- **Scoreboard (#185).** `score_sort` (score, kills, deaths, ping, name; within each team) and `score_cols` (a bit per column).
- **Alerts (#241).** `alerts`: with the window in the background the taskbar button flashes on a match start, a round start, a vote called or a player joining (`glfwRequestWindowAttention`).
- **On join (#243).** `on_join` play / spectate / ask, applied once when a match's first snapshot arrives.
- **Quips (#80, #130).** Pause > PLAYERS is now open to every player (KICK, bots and restart stay the host's). QUIPS ON / OFF per player writes `quip_block` (names), and their quips are not played. `quip_pick` (PLAYER > MORE: MY QUIP) sends `quip <n>` to the server, which plays that one instead of a random one. No wheel: a picker only.
- **Hide ratings, remember queues (#171).** `hide_ratings` drops the RATING column from the ranked scoreboard; `remember_queues` saves your ranked queues (`last_queues`) and queues them again at the next start (not in the UI test). Not done: fill notify / join, Discord presence and showing Discord names (each needs the Discord bot or Worker side).

**Spectating and the death camera**
- **Death camera (#75, #155).** `death_cam` the killer / my body / a teammate, for `kill_cam_time` 0 to 3 s (the client default stays 1.5; the server's limit `kKillCamTime` was raised from 1.5 to 3 so a longer camera isn't cut off).
- **Spectating (#32).** `spec_fov` and `spec_third` (third person behind the watched player, pulled in by a ray against the map so it never goes through a wall). Free camera already existed. Demo speed and seek are not done: playback runs through the snapshot clock, so a speed needs the interpolation clock scaled with it, and seeking back needs a replay from the start with events muted; more than a setting.

**Controls (CONTROLS > MORE 2, binds)**
- **Per weapon (#40, #142, #143).** `wpn_sens_<n>` (x0.1 to x5, written only when not 1), `alt_toggle_weapons` (a bit per weapon; -1 = the old rule, the scopes only) and `swap_fire` (a bit per weapon: fire and alt-fire swapped while it's held).
- **Mouse (#40).** `mouse_accel` 0 to 1 (faster moves turn further, at most x4) and `raw_input` (GLFW raw mouse motion where supported; off: the OS's pointer motion).
- **Auto-hop (#146).** `auto_hop` off: holding jump jumps once; let go and press again.
- **Console (#123).** `console_key` (` F1 F10 F11 INSERT HOME \ '; one no action is bound to) and `console_menus` (open it from the menus too).
- **Ranked (#150).** `ranked_alt_toggle`: the alt-fire (scope) toggle allowed in ranked; the other control scripts stay off there.
- **New binds (#235, #237).** VOL UP / VOL DOWN / MUTE, RECORD (start / stop a demo), JOIN TEAM, SPECTATE, appended to the action list so older binds keep their slots.
- **Mode profiles (#126).** A controls profile can now hold `sensitivity`, `fov`, `zoom_fov_sniper` and `zoom_fov_rifle`, applied while that mode is played; EXPORT writes them. Crosshair per mode is not done: the crosshair is the other worker's tab.

**Menus (PLAYER > MORE, #52, #107, #118, #131)**
- `menu_theme` dark / high contrast (buttons outlined, brighter text), `menu_accent_on` + `menu_accent` (not the team colour), `text_bright`, `panel_dark`. No light theme: the menus are drawn over the 3D backdrop and every hint colour assumes a dark panel; it needs a design pass.
- `menu_bg` clockwork / a map you pick (`menu_bg_map`) / a plain colour (`menu_bg_color`).
- The data folder's path with OPEN (MOVE not done: moving a running game's data folder needs a restart and a copy step).
- Mods: `use_overrides` (your own asset overrides on or off, applied at start), buttons for the overrides and mods folders, and how many overrides you have. No live reload: assets are loaded once at start.

**HUD elements and preview (#21, #128, #26)**
- **Objective, notices and scoreboard as HUD elements (#21).** `HUD_OBJECTIVE`, `HUD_NOTICES`, `HUD_SCOREBOARD` are appended (hud_11 to hud_13), so older configs' hud_<n> keep their meaning, and theme.cfg gets `objective`, `notices` and `scoreboard`. They keep their own drawing code (the stacked notice column and the scoreboard's table); `Renderer::uiTransform` moves and sizes what they drew, as a group, about their anchor (the objective line's top centre, the notices' top centre, the scoreboard's top centre), plus the theme's margin and opacity. At the defaults nothing runs (one compare). The objective's colour tints its line; NOTICES tints the notices and the round message; the scoreboard keeps its team colours, so it has no colour row. Hidden: the objective line, its bar and the bomb timer go; the scoreboard isn't drawn on its key. Ranked keeps the default HUD. They're on Settings > HUD's ELEMENT list; the on-screen editor doesn't list them yet (it needs each element's box, which a fixed-code draw doesn't give).
- **Model preview (#128).** PLAYER shows the chosen model in your team colour under the outlines, turning slowly; drag to turn it. Its boxes (`playerModelParts`, or a box override) are projected flat into the 2D batch (back faces culled, far faces first, one light): no render target, no extra pass, about 150 quads, and only while the tab is open. A mesh override shows a line instead.
- **Widget values (#26).** hud.cfg widgets can show `snaprate`, `accuracy`, `damage`, `streak`, `round`, `vspeed`, `height` and `weapon` (appended to the value list). Per-mode widgets already work through the HUD profiles (HUD > PER MODE).

**Host and votes**
- **Votes (#187).** callvote `restart`, `extend` (5 more minutes), `shuffle` (the humans dealt to the teams alternately, then a restart) and `kick <player>` (the same checks as moving one to spectator; the target can't vote). Text commands and vote text, so no protocol change.
- **Host advanced (#188, #189).** `host_tick` and `host_snap` (cycles), `host_upload` (an upload cap), `host_auto_start`, `host_bot_skill` (balance bot skill): passed to the server config at host start. Tourney rotation not done: it needs the bracket to carry over between matches on the server.
- **Vs bots (#192).** Up to 15 enemies and 15 teammates (was 7 / 7).
- **Not done:** a mode rules tab from the param table (#190) and the custom mode editor's extras (#191: weapon set, movement, logic file, duplicate, share code). Both change the custom mode format and the host flow; they need q's call on the share code format.

**Skipped, with why**
- #76 pause offline games when unfocused: the server's pause resets the match state and the snapshot stream; needs a server-side pause first.
- #133 practice speed: needs a timescale through prediction and the server tick.
- #170 UTF-8 chat: the font atlas is 8-bit and the server sanitises to ASCII.
- #184 leaderboard paging / around me: needs new Worker API endpoints.
- #238 instant replay buffer: needs an always-on in-memory recorder; left for a performance review.
- #127 player accent colour: other players would need it, which is a protocol change.

## Servers: testing-only install (owner, 2026-10-30: "default is both, you should have the option")

- **`install.sh --testing-only`** sets up only the testing instance (user `quark-testing`, `/opt/quark-testing`, `/var/lib/quark-testing`) on the production port (`--port`, default 27500; `--testing-port` overrides). No `quark` user or production clone is made; an existing production server is stopped and disabled with its data kept. The testing instance builds this checkout itself (there's no production build to copy) and runs its first update check during the install, as production does.
- **Always on.** Its update unit sets `QUARK_TESTING_ALWAYS=1`, and `autoupdate.sh` then skips the "only while newer than production" switch: no `active` file, no `quark-testing-active.path`, no `ExecCondition`. It runs the newest testing build, and installs and runs the production release once that is newer (the updater already built production in that case to keep its own code current; now the server also runs it), so the box never sits empty after a release. Why: a box meant only for testing would otherwise be idle from each release until the next testing push.
- **No governor, normal priority** when alone: the load governor and `Nice=5` / `CPUWeight=50` exist to make room for production, which isn't there.
- **Remembered** in `/etc/quark/install.conf` (`PRODUCTION=0`), so re-running install.sh with no options keeps it, taking the server options from the testing unit. `--both` goes back to both (the testing port then moves to production + 1 again unless `--testing-port` says otherwise); `--no-testing` still means production only. The default is unchanged.

## Modes: power (MODE_POWER 14; owner, 2026-10-30: "each team has player count * 2500 health, nobody can die, just soak damage; when total team health reaches 0 they lose")

- **Rules.** Team vs with one health pool per team: `poolPerPlayer` (2500, a new param of the new `pool` object) x the players on it, full at the start of each round. In `Server::damagePlayer`, an enemy's hit (after the ruleset's multipliers, so instagib takes 101) comes off the victim's team pool while the round is live, and never off the player's own health: nobody dies of damage, and knockback lands as before. Your own hits, teammates' and the world's (acid, spike floors and the other liquid / feature damage through `takeAcid`) take nothing, so a rocket jump or a lava touch can't lose the match for your team; a fall out of the map still kills and respawns you after 2 s (`modeRespawnDelay`) at no cost to the pool. A pool at 0 loses the round. When the 600 s clock runs out, the pool with the larger share of its size wins (a draw starts another round). One round wins the match (`rounds` 1).
- **Joins and leaves** (`poolStep`, every tick): a player who joins a team mid-round adds `poolPerPlayer` to its pool and its size; one who leaves takes their share off the size, and the pool shrinks to fit it (an emptied team's pool goes to 0 and it loses).
- **Wire and demos.** The pools ride in ModeState fields power doesn't otherwise use (`powerPool` / `powerPoolMax`, protocol.h: payloadDist / payloadLen = the pools, point / pointCount = the players each counts, target = hp per player), so the snapshot format and every demo stay as they were. Protocol 54 anyway: an older client has no mode 14 (demos convert with no change).
- **Mode logic.** `tools/modes/power.qlogic` (`use pool`, `poolStep()`, the `pool[team]` (rw) and `poolMax[team]` arrays) says what the native rules do; the lockstep test plays power on Bastion natively and through the interpreter at 1000 / 250 / 125 Hz and finds no difference. Custom modes can base on power.
- **HUD.** The score box shows your pool and the enemy's (rounded up) instead of rounds; the objective line reads "TEAM POOL n / size - ENEMY n / size" with your pool's share as its bar. Main menu > INFO > MODES explains it. The mode pickers suggest Bastion (power plays on the team vs map).
- **Maps.** Bastion and Orbital (team vs) and Lodestone also offer power (`also` in their builder files), so they're graded for it like their other modes.
- **Bots** play it as team vs (no objective; they fight).
- **Tests.** `testPower` (rules): the pools start at 2500 each; an 80 hit takes 80 from the victim's pool and none of their health; your own rocket takes nothing; a third player brings 2500 to their team; emptying team B's pool gives team A the round. The every-mode bot run plays power on Bastion; the lockstep covers it.
