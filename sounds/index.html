    Use your sound in the game

    Pick the sound it replaces; the download is named after it (HIT.wav, 48 kHz mono). Then put it in one of two places:

    • Just for you: overrides/sounds/default/HIT.wav in your user folder (Windows %APPDATA%/quark, macOS ~/Library/Application Support/quark, Linux ~/.local/share/quark). The game reads it at startup.
    • For everyone, in a checkout of the game: assets/overrides/sounds/default/HIT.wav. It's built into the game and ships with the next release.

    The game makes it mono, trims silence at the start, cuts it at 3 s (announcer lines at 5 s) and plays it as loud as the sound it replaces, so the kits stay fair. A sound for the DEFAULT kit also replaces that sound in every kit that used DEFAULT's (RETRO gets it chiptuned). Your own mods/sounds/ still win over it. WAV, OGG, FLAC and MP3 all work. The full rules are in docs/ASSETS.md.
