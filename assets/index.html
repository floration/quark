    Use your model in the game

    Pick what it replaces; the download is named after it (for example STANDARD.glb). Then put it in one of two places:

    • Just for you: overrides/models/player/STANDARD.glb in your user folder (Windows %APPDATA%/quark, macOS ~/Library/Application Support/quark, Linux ~/.local/share/quark). The game reads it at startup.
    • For everyone, in a checkout of the game: assets/overrides/models/player/STANDARD.glb. It's built into the game and ships with the next release.

    Metres, +Y up, front at -Z. Players stand on the origin, 2 m tall, and share one hitbox: stay inside the default's outline. A box marked team colour is exported white in a material named team, which the game paints in the team colour (your accent colour on a weapon, gold or silver on the crown). Up to 20,000 triangles (each box is 12). The full rules are in docs/ASSETS.md.
