Update

quark 1.1.0

Everything that changed in this version, and why.

The full notes for this version are its short notes: that was all that was written then.

Ranked race: Viaduct time trials.

  • TIME TRIAL (main menu, or the leaderboard): a solo race on Viaduct, on your own machine with no other players needed.
    • Each round is one run, and they repeat for as long as you like.
    • The clock at the top shows your run time.
    • Every checkpoint pops up your split and how far ahead (green) or behind (red) your best run you are.
    • Time trials use the ranked fairness rules: default model, HUD, controls and 10 ms interpolation.
  • Leaderboard: a new VIADUCT TIME TRIAL column shows the fastest times. Only a new personal best is sent, and the server also keeps only your best.
    • Every submitted run includes the time at each checkpoint, so suspicious runs can be checked and deleted by hand (see tools/master/README.md).
    • The website shows the board too.
  • MY RACE STATS (on the leaderboard): every Viaduct run you finish, in a time trial or a normal race, is kept in race_times.log. The page shows:
    • your runs and best time;
    • your best possible time, the sum of your best segments;
    • your average and your average over the last 10 runs;
    • each checkpoint on your best run, and how much time it loses against your best segment there;
    • your recent runs, each with a bar against your best.
  • Race rounds now last 5 minutes (Viaduct is two loops). Protocol 21.

For the server owner: the ranking Worker needs the new table and code before times show up. It takes two steps in the Cloudflare dashboard; see tools/master/README.md → "Race time trials".
