Update

quark 0.27.1

Everything that changed in this version, and why.

The full notes for this version are its short notes: that was all that was written then.

  • You spawn holding slot 1 (rocket), or the first slot the mode allows.
  • Laser Vampire healing fades over a round: 100% of the damage you deal at the start, down to 50% after one minute, then it stays at 50%. Even fights end instead of both players healing forever.
  • Knife throw: after you throw, the knife is gone for 5 seconds.
    • You switch straight to your next weapon with no delay on it, and can't pull the knife back out early.
    • The weapon list shows a countdown next to KNIFE.
    • In knives-only modes you keep stabbing; only the next throw waits.
    • Bots handle this too.
  • The network protocol changed, so servers and clients must both be on 0.27.1.
