# Map design: one mode per map, and why every structure is there

Every map is built for **one** mode (its `for` line; menus pick it for that mode). Below each map:
- the mode it's built for;
- **why** its layout suits that mode;
- what every structure on it is for.

A structure earns its place for the game (a route, cover, a perch, a loop, a sightline breaker) or for the place (it explains what the map is). If neither applies, it goes.

The numbers in brackets were measured by the map report `quark_tests` used to grade maps with (removed: the owner is replacing the generated maps with human-made ones); they're kept as the notes they were, not re-measured. The maps are generated by `tools/make_maps.py`: each map's own code is `tools/maps/<name>.py`, the shared builder and prefabs are `tools/maps/prefabs.py`.

What every map gets, whatever its mode (checked by `quark_tests`):
- **A spawn per player.** Every mode a map offers has enough spawns for a full 8-player server in the pool the server really draws from: 8 "any" spawns for free for all, hide, tag and a tourney's warm-up, 8 race spawns behind a race's start line, 4 a side in team modes, 1.5 m apart at least. The generator tops short pools up with spread-out spots on open floor the existing spawns can walk to.
- **Spawns that face into the map** (2 m clear ahead at eye height), and in team modes **out of sight of the enemy's spawns**: where a long lane ran spawn to spawn, a screen in front of the spawn row closes it (you leave round its ends).
- **Every route walkable**: from every spawn to every objective (and to the enemy, or to every other free-for-all spawn), with the player's own hull against the map's collision, no movement tech needed.
- **A clear track**: nothing stands in the payload cart's way; the generator keeps its sweep (and other keep-clear volumes, like a race course's openings) free of anything placed by hand or by the dressing passes.
- **Ranked maps are fair, not necessarily symmetric** (q, 2026-10-30: "would it be fair even though it's asymmetrical?"): on a ladder played 1v1 each side has as many spawns, and on a plain run both sides are within 1 s of each other to the enemy spawns and to the middle of the map. The qk maps and Dojo are point-symmetric anyway (their spawns stay in mirrored pairs, the sun overhead); Greenhouse and Spire aren't.
- **Ladders and stairs that work**: every ladder is climbed and every staircase walked up with the real movement code, and no staircase cuts through another walkable surface or hangs over one with less than a player's height of clearance.
- **Nothing out of bounds**: from the first spawn, with every move a good player has (bunny-hops, dashes, the grapple, measured with the game's movement code), no floor outside the playable floor's footprint is reachable (`quark_tests bounds`; `OOB_TRACE=<map>` lists what is).
- **One mode and a setting**: a `for <mode>` line the map offers, and a `setting` line.

`quark_tests maps` (or `group maps`) runs them all but the out-of-bounds check, which is `quark_tests bounds` (the full suite runs it as two groups, `mapbounds` and `mapbounds2`, every other map each). **Human-made maps** (a `handmade` line, which the editor writes on every save; a file `tools/make_maps.py` didn't write; or an override in `assets/overrides/maps/`) run every check, but what they miss is printed as `HUMAN-MADE` and doesn't fail the run. `LADDER_TRACE=<map>` (with `LADDER_AT=<x>`) and `STAIRS_TRACE=<map>` trace a climb.

**Black Hole** is built for the 3D VAMP ruleset (zero-g flight, lasers): a flat 40 x 20 m asteroid top with no cover at all (the fight is in the air round it, so cover would only be something to orbit), the rock tapering away under it in layers so its underside has no ledges to stand on. Everything else is scenery: the black hole (far boxes), its lensed ring and accretion disc (lava: bright and not solid, so the map's bounds, and with them the flyers' 40 m kill box, stay round the rock). `void -50`; `holdfire 3`: the spawns face each other across the bare floor, so nobody fires for the first 3 s.

## Tutorial (1.19.9): the movement tutorial's own map

**Tutorial** (`tools/maps/tutorial.py`, `for ffa`) is never in a map list, vote or picker (hidden by name: `mapHiddenFromLists`, map.h); only MAIN MENU > TUTORIAL hosts it (src/client/tutorial.cpp, src/game/tutorial.h). It's a **training hall**, not an arena: a sealed 24 m-wide bay per lesson, side by side along z in the lessons' order. Every bay starts at x -90 and runs along +x, so the whole map stays inside the snapshots' +-512 m. A row along x ran to 1137 m, and the ghost was drawn stuck at x 512. Each bay has two lanes: the ghost's 6 m left of the middle and yours 6 m right, both starting 3 m in on a trim pad and facing +x, with a trim strip between the lanes and a line across every 10 m to read distance by. Nothing joins the bays: the tutorial moves you, so a lesson can't be skipped by walking, and nobody wanders into another tech's room.

Why each bay is the shape it is (the bay lengths and heights are `kLessons` in src/game/tutorial.h; `quark_tests tutorial` runs every lesson's ghost on this map and fails if the map stops working for it):
- **Walking** (50 x 12): two metal hurdles (0.8 m: a jump, not a step) and a glowing finish line at 44 m.
- **AD strafing** (150 m) and **super chaining** (180 m, 16 m tall: hypers rise 2-3 m): long and empty. Strafing needs room to build speed by turning, and the chain gets to 34 m/s.
- **Dash** (50): a 9 m lava pit (16-25 m in, hazard-striped edges, its bottom 6 m down). A running jump falls short and a jump with an air dash clears it. Touching the lava puts you back at the start.
- **Sideways dashes** (30), **super** and **hyper** (60), **dash slide** (60), **grapple walking** (80): flat and long enough for the tech and its landing.
- **Wall bounce** (12): short, its end wall hazard-striped. That's the wall to dash into, 9 m from the start.
- **Grapple** (40 x 18): a 10 m ledge 18 m in with a flat face (a 5 cm overhang caught the rope) and a striped edge. The hook takes the ceiling over it.
- **Rocket jump** and **sticky jump** (40 x 24): an 8 m ledge 14 m in. A jump plus a blast reaches it and a plain jump doesn't.
- **The launch** (160 x 72, KEY TECH): the tall hall. The launch goes 50 m up and 65 m along, so it needs the height and the run-up for the grapple walk. Rows of lights and glow bands every 16 m up its walls keep it lit to the top.
- **Rocket riding** (110 x 16): long, for the ride along the lane.

It has 8 "any" spawns at the first bay's start (a match on it, from the console, still works). `quark_tests` checks it like every map: spawns, walk routes, nothing out of bounds.

## Legacy maps (the pool before 1.19.6)

Clockwork, Depot, Summit, Canal, Harbor, Bastion, Foundry, Temple, Colosseum, Furnace, Gantry, Anvil, Viaduct, Hamlet, Downtown, Orbital, Lodestone, Midtown, Hightown and Cascade are still built (`tools/maps/<name>.py`, the LEGACY list in `tools/make_maps.py`) into `maps/legacy/` and shipped there. They aren't official (never ranked, not embedded) and the menus list them only with Settings > GAMEPLAY > LEGACY MAPS on; asked for by name they always load. Their design notes are in this file's history (1.19.5 and earlier).
