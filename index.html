QUARK

A fast, minimal multiplayer shooter on a fully custom engine.

Two colors, baked light, black outlines on every edge, and an engine and netcode where latency comes first. Free on Windows 7–11, macOS and Linux.

Download launcher What's new

The engine

Built for frame times, not frame rates.

quark is C++ from the ground up with no third-party engine. Every system is judged by its worst frames (1% and 0.1% lows), not its average.

NETCODE

Predicted, lag-compensated

Client prediction with replay, 3-snapshot interpolation and server-side rewind for every hitscan shot. Shot spread is seeded, so your tracer shows exactly what the server hit.

RENDERER

Baked light, live shadows

Pre-baked sun, sky and bounce lighting, real-time shadows on players and projectiles, and outlines computed in shaders. No textures, just shades of grey.

LATENCY

Low-latency mode on any GPU

Exclusive or borderless fullscreen, every present mode, and a Reflex-style mode that works on any GPU. The F3 overlay shows input-to-frame latency.

MOVEMENT

Bhop, dash, grapple

Hold jump to bunny-hop, dash on Shift (it tilts with your aim), and a momentum grapple on Q. Strong air control throughout.

PLAY

Bots, votes, server list

Four bot levels that aim like people, balance bots for uneven teams, votes for start, map, mode and bots, and an internet server list.

LAUNCHER

Always up to date

The launcher installs quark, keeps it updated, and lets you pin any older version. It also adds desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and an entry in installed programs.

Game modes

Eight modes, each with a map built for it.

Every mode also runs on Arena, and players can vote to change the map or mode mid-session.

Free for all

FOUNDRY

Everyone for themselves. A tie at the time limit goes to sudden death.

Duel

CRUCIBLE

1v1 or 2v2. Every life gets a random modifier: vampire laser, rockets only, knives only, 1 HP, double size, or grapple-only movement.

VS

DUEL

Team rounds with one life each. Stalemates drain health.

Bomb

DEPOT

Attackers plant at one of two sites; defenders defuse.

King of the hill

SUMMIT

Hold the plateau to score. First team to the target wins.

Payload

RAILYARD

Push the solid cart down the track. Each team attacks once, and the faster push wins.

Neutral payload

TUG

One cart in the middle. Push it into the enemy's end.

Race

SPRINT

Checkpoints in order. Weapons only knock players back.

Hosting

Host from the game or run a dedicated server.

IN GAME

Host game

Pick a map and mode and press Start. UPnP opens the port on your router automatically. Leave PUBLIC on to show the game on everyone's server list. Friends can also join with your public IP and port (UDP 27500).

DEDICATED
