temple. it's the one we play the most

it's a fast movement shooter in the quake / diabotical family. you get a grappling hook, a dash, rocket jumps and bunny hops, and a pile of weapons that all feel a bit unfair in their own way. i wrote it from scratch in c++ because i wanted it to feel instant: the server runs at 1000 hz and the game cares more about frame time spikes than average fps.

it's free and open source, and you can host your own server from the menu.

what's in it

  • 12 modes: ffa, team vs, duel (30 dumb modifiers, a new one every life), 1v1 rulesets, tourney brackets, bomb, payload, neutral payload, king of the hill, race, hide and seek, and tag
  • ranked 1v1 on three ladders (vanilla, laser vampire, instagib)
  • viaduct time trials: solo runs for the race leaderboard. it shows splits so you can see exactly where you lost time
  • a map editor inside the game, and you can edit maps together with friends over the network
  • bots from "beginner" to "why is it doing that"
  • a hud editor where you drag widgets around and pick what they show, plus snap tap / null binds, player models and sound packs

ranked and time trials always use the default model, hud, controls and 10 ms interp. nobody gets a weird edge.

the hud editor. yes you can put your health bar in the middle of the screen if you want

run a server

hosting from the game menu is the easy way. for a 24/7 one on a linux box (a free cloud vm is plenty):

sh tools/server/install.sh --public --name "my server"
# ranked:  --ranked --variant vanilla --map temple

it starts on boot, restarts if it crashes, and updates itself when nobody's mid-match.
