QUARK

A fast, free arena shooter.

Download Discord

What it is

  • 1000 Hz servers, movement that plays the same at any frame rate.
  • Dash, grapple, rocket jumps. Rifle, rockets, knife, sniper, laser, shotgun, stickies, crossbow.
  • Free for all, team modes, bomb, payload, king of the hill, race, 1v1 and tourneys.
  • Ranked 1v1 ladders, a map editor you can use together, and anyone can host.

The launcher installs the game and keeps it up to date.
