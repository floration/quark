a tiny multiplayer shooter on a homemade engine.
no textures. two colors. black lines on everything. fast as hell.

./get_quark download

The launcher installs the game, keeps it updated, and lets you roll back to any old version if I break something. (I will break something.)

what is this

quark is an arena FPS written from scratch in C++. No Unity, no Unreal, no engine. Just a pile of code that draws grey boxes really, really quickly.

  • the server ticks at 1000 Hz. movement runs in 1 ms steps, so it plays the same at any frame rate or tick rate.
  • input-to-frame latency is under half a millisecond with low-latency mode on. hit F3 in game and it'll show you.
  • bunny hop by holding space, dash on shift (aim up to hop it), and a grappling hook on Q that keeps your momentum.
  • lighting is baked (sun, sky, one bounce). players and rockets get real shadows.
  • bots in 4 flavours of bad, from "can't hit a barn" to "honestly kind of rude".
  • runs on Windows 7 through 11, macOS and Linux. probably runs on your toaster too.
pro tip: the 0.1% low fps is the number that matters. the average is a liar.

modes

Every mode has a map built for it. You can also vote to change the map or mode mid-game, democracy style.

free for allfoundry, harbor
1v1 (8 rulesets)temple: vampire, instagib, moon shotgun...
tourneycolosseum: a bracket of everyone, winner stays
duelcrucible: 1v1 or 2v2, cursed modifier every life
vsduel: one life each, stalemates drain hp
bombdepot: plant it or defuse it
king of the hillsummit: stand on the thing
payloadrailyard: push the cart
neutral payloadtug: push the cart the other way
racesprint: rocket jump to the finish

guns

Eight of them. This table gets pulled straight out of the source code every release, so if I nerf something you'll see it here first.

you@quark:~$ cat src/game/gamedefs.h | ./ttk --sort
loading...

ttk = time to kill a full-health player if you never miss. Splash falls off with distance. Your own rocket only does 10% to you when the blast is under your feet (rocket jump away). Laser alt-fire is a tractor beam. The dark grey walls are wallbangable: hitscan goes through them for half damage.

hosting

Hit HOST GAME in the menu. UPnP tries to open your router port by itself, and if PUBLIC is on, your game shows up in everyone's server list. Up to 32 players (8 by default, because 32 at 1000 Hz is a lot of packets).

Or run the dedicated server that comes in every download:

$ quark_server --mode tourney --map colosseum --public --upnp $ quark_server --mode 1v1 --variant instagib --max 2 $ quark_server --mode ffa --map harbor --max 16 --log simple

If UPnP fails, forward UDP 27500 on your router. If that fails too, your ISP probably has you behind CGNAT. Sorry.
